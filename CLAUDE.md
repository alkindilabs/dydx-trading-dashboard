## Chart Explanations

### Trading Activity by Market
The Trading Activity chart (doughnut chart on Overview tab) shows:
- Distribution of your trades across different markets (ETH-USD, BTC-USD, etc.)
- Each slice size = number of trades in that market (CLOSED count); a market with no closed position takes no slice
- Labels show market name and trade count, followed by the open count only when it is non-zero (`+ N open`) or unknown (`+ — open` while the OPEN rows are unknown)
- Legend shows each market's percentage of total trading activity: its closed positions over the closed positions of every market, including those beyond the five shown. After the slices it lists every market holding open positions but no closed one (or whose open count is unknown), with a hollow swatch, its open count and its profit (`—` while unknown, the reasons on hover), since such a market has no slice to hover
- Hover tooltip displays:
  - Number of trades and percentage
  - Profit for that market labeled "Profit (incl. funding − fees)" = FIFO realized + FIFO unrealized of the lots left open (at the oracle) + netFunding − fees across every fill in the market (same definition as Performance-by-Asset PROFIT column AND the Total Profit headline); reads `— (reason)` when that market's profit is gated, with the same reason as the asset table
  - Number of open positions when non-zero, or `— (reason)` while the OPEN rows are unknown
- Shows top 5 most traded markets (by closed count)
- While the closed counts are unknown (`processData()`'s `closedTradesGap`: the CLOSED list failed, or the fills hold a closed trade no listed position owns), passed as `AppCharts.market.render(marketDistribution, closedCountGap)`, the chart draws no slice and the legend's only item reads `— <reason>` with the reason on hover: no market is shown as a share of, or as having no, closed positions on a count that is unknown
- Helps identify trading focus and diversification

### Cumulative Profit
The Overview candlestick chart (`AppCharts.pnl.render(historicalPnl, live, historyCut, gap)`) buckets the anchored `totalPnl` series by month (span over 350 days), week (over 50) or day, in UTC. A candle's tooltip title names the period it covers, so its Close (the bucket's last point, the live point on the last candle) never reads as the value on the bucket's first day: a month by name (`Mar 2025`, as its tick), a week as its Monday to Sunday (`2025-03-03 – 2025-03-09`), a day as its date. Its "Below peak:" line is the deepest point below the running all-time peak inside the bucket, measured and ranked as displayed (see Headline Max Drawdown, One displayed drawdown: the displayed running peak less the displayed point), not the bucket's own drawdown from the prior bucket's close (the Monthly MAX DD), so a flat month long under water reads a "Below peak" amount beside a MAX DD of `$0`. `processData()` renders it on every load with `gap`, `historicalPnlSeriesGap` in `index.html`: the `/historical-pnl` missing-input reason, else "No /historical-pnl rows" when there are none ('' with rows). Every call first destroys the previous chart, so a load never keeps an earlier load's or the cached snapshot's candles; with a gap, or a series of fewer than two points ("Not enough /historical-pnl rows to chart"), the chart's empty state (`#pnlCumulativeChartEmpty`, hidden otherwise) reads `—` and the reason. A candle is coloured by its open and close as displayed at whole dollars (`candleDirection`, private to `src/charts/pnl-chart.js`): up when the close reads above the open, down when below, the unchanged colour when the two read alike, so a sub-dollar move never colours a candle whose tooltip shows the same Open and Close. Each candle hands chartjs-chart-financial its own colour through the plugin's `backgroundColors` / `borderColors` options, since the plugin itself compares the raw values.

### Funding Rate · Price History
The Market tab's chart for the selected market (`src/charts/funding-rate-chart.js`; fetching, caching and the missing-price-line behaviour: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Funding chart):
- Bars are the hourly funding rate in percent; the line is the hourly close price (a rate or close that is not wholly numeric, `RiskMetrics.decimalNumberOf`, draws nothing), unsigned (see Sign convention), plotted at the instant the candle closes: each bar carries the close of the candle that ended at its settlement (`buildPriceLine`), so a rate and the price beside it in the tooltip are the same instant. The All window reaches back `AppConstants.FUNDING_CHART_MAX_DAYS` days, and the subtitle reads that constant (`#fundingChartMaxDays`) rather than repeating the number.
- Funding-rate axis labels take their decimals from the tick step (`rateTickLabel`, exposed on the chart's `_internal` for tests), so adjacent ticks never print alike at realistic hourly rates; a label that prints as zero reads `0%`.
- The x axis reads UTC, the clock funding settles on, like the tooltip title, the hero Period and the CURRENT cell: ticks fall on UTC hours, UTC midnights or UTC Mondays (`afterBuildTicks`), each labelled in UTC (`Sep 23, 17:00` / `Sep 23`), and the axis is titled `Time (UTC)`, whatever the viewer's timezone.
- The tooltip shows the hourly rate through `Format.formatHourlyRate` and its annualized value, hourly rate × `AppConstants.HOURS_PER_YEAR`.

**Hourly rates.** Every hourly funding rate on the Market tab (the chart tooltip, the CURRENT / PREDICTED cell tooltips through `Format.formatHourlyDetail`, the hero's Implied hourly) prints through `Format.formatHourlyRate`, trailing zeros trimmed, with at least `HOURLY_RATE_STEP_DECIMALS` (7) decimals in percent and at least `HOURLY_RATE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` (3) significant digits: dYdX rates step in 1.25e-7 (0.0000125%), so every multiple of the step prints exactly (`+0.0000125%`, `+0.0001125%`, `-0.0007625%`, whose × 8760 is the `-6.68%` annualized beside it), never rounded or a false `0%`, and a rate off the step (a predicted rate, the hero's average) keeps three significant digits however small, rounded half away from zero like every number on screen.

### Funding rate of return, funding cards and Funding Rate Analysis by Asset
The Market tab's other funding views (`src/panels/market.js`) read the `/fundingPayments` rows inside the selected window (7D / 30D / 90D / All), one list for all of them (`paymentsInWindow`); the hero also reads `/fills`. Every window ends at the snapshot time `processData()` received (`snapshotAtMs`: the cache's `fetchedAt` on hydrate, else the fetch time), never at the clock: `renderMarketTab(data, snapshotAtMs)` passes it to `AppPanels.market.render(payments, marketsMap, gaps, fills, snapshotAtMs)`, which keeps it for the hero's repaints and the window pills, so a settlement after the snapshot (its payment row not in the snapshot) falls outside every window. Membership is by settlement hour index (`floor(settlement instant / 1 h)`), the same for the cards, the table and the hero: a payment row and a held hour belong to the window when that hour lies in `[firstWindowHour(), lastSettledHour()]`. The last hour is the last whose settlement came at or before the snapshot (by a loaded `/historicalFunding` walk that reaches that hour, else the hour rule), so a settlement that had not happened by the snapshot leaves its hour out; a 7D / 30D / 90D window is the fixed span of the days × 24 settlement hours ending there (`first = last − days × 24 + 1`, always 168 / 720 / 2160 hours, whatever the snapshot's minute), and the All window has no start. A walk that lands and moves the last hour moves the first with it, so the hero, the cards and the table are repainted on the moved window's payments (`repaintIfWindowMoved`). A row whose settlement drifted past the end of its hour stays with that hour:
- **Hero exposure.** dYdX v4 settles funding at every hour, but the indexer writes a `/fundingPayments` row only when funding moved (a rate-0 settlement leaves none), so the hero's settlements come from the position, not the rows (`settledExposure`). Each settlement is its own exposure unit: after a chain halt several settlements can land in one clock hour, and each counts. A market is held at a settlement when a payment row pays it, or when the fills put its net size off zero just before the settlement (`RiskMetrics.netSizeHistory`, the attribution's FIFO walk). "Just before" is the settlement's own instant, the `effectiveAt` of its `/historicalFunding` row, once a loaded walk reaches back to that hour (`hourSettlements`, every listed settlement in the hour, oldest first), so a settlement that drifted past a close is not held and one that came after an open earlier in that hour is; until the settlements load (or for an hour the walk did not reach) the hour is one settlement at the hour itself. In an hour with several settlements, each payment row of the market pays one of them, the latest not yet paid at or before its own instant, else the earliest not yet paid (`paidSettlements`); the rest are judged by the fills. Hours deployed therefore shows at once on the hour rule and is corrected when the settlements land, while APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly wait for them (below). Any window hour without a row in which the fills put a market off zero at some moment (`unrowedHoursFrom`), a position opened and closed inside the hour included, needs that market's settlements, since the settlement may have come while it was open although it is flat at both hour marks. A settlement with a row takes the row's |size| × oracle price; one without takes the fills' |net size| at that settlement's own oracle price, the `price` of its `/historicalFunding` row, and moved no funding (the two weigh alike: one notional per market per settlement); never a payment row's price from another hour, which may belong to an earlier, closed position. An hour `/historicalFunding` lists no settlement in was not settled (during a chain halt or slowdown the funding ticks drift off the hour), so an hour that market's own loaded walk, requested since the snapshot, reaches but lists no settlement in leaves the exposure, whatever another market's walk shows (`hourSettlements` decides per market and hour); the hour rule counts only an hour no such walk reached. A listed settlement is a settlement whatever its price: every `/historicalFunding` row with a valid `effectiveAt` decides whether its hour was settled (the held-hour judgement and the window's last hour read `effectiveAt` alone), and a held hour without a row whose settlement has no positive price stays in Hours deployed and Time in market while APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly read `—` with "<market> settlement at <hour> UTC has no oracle price" (the earliest such hour). Settlement hours in a 7D / 30D / 90D window are its fixed span of hour indexes (above), and that count is the window's hours, so held hours never exceed it and a position held at every settlement reads 100.0%.
- **Settlement prices.** The settlements come from one `/historicalFunding` walk per market with a position in an hour without a row, back to its earliest such hour (`loadFundingHistory`), fetched only while the Market tab is open and shared with the funding chart: a walk in flight or fresh that reaches back far enough and was requested at or after the snapshot (`requestedAt`) is reused, so the chart's 90-day walk serves a 7D / 30D / 90D hero. Only a walk requested at or after the snapshot can show that a settlement had not happened by it (one requested earlier cannot list a settlement between the two): an earlier walk never ends the window before the snapshot's hour (the hour rule applies), counts as not loaded for the hero ("… load while this tab is open" on a closed tab), and is refetched rather than reused, while the chart may keep drawing it until the refetch lands; a walk requested earlier that lands after a later one never replaces it. Until they are loaded, APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly read `—` with the reason in the caption (`ORACLE_PRICE_NOTES`: "… load while this tab is open", "Fetching settlement oracle prices…", "… failed to load"), while Hours deployed, Time in market and Period still show. The walk starts at the indexer's present, so it is sized from the clock, not the snapshot, and for the indexer's inclusive cursor (`DydxApi.fetchHistoricalFunding`: every page after the first repeats one row), and the cache records the hour of the oldest settlement it returned (`reachedHour`); a walk that ended after the market's first hour without a row (it hit its page cap, or the indexer had no older row) is short: the same three figures read `—` with "<market> settlement history does not reach back to <hour> UTC, …", and no held hour older than its oldest settlement is dropped as unsettled. A settlement `/historicalFunding` lists without a parseable price (absent, blank or not wholly numeric: `60000zz` is no price) still settled its hour: the hour stays held (Hours deployed, Time in market, Period) and, as the newest settlement, still ends the window, while APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly read `—` with "<market> settlement at <hour> UTC has no oracle price" (the earliest such held hour without a payment row). Known limitation: a market whose every held hour already has a payment row needs no walk (`unrowedHoursFrom` does not list it), so the hero does not load its `/historicalFunding`, and a rate-0 settlement that a chain halt left in an hour that also holds another settlement's row is not seen: that settlement adds no notional, and Avg notional and APR read as though it had not happened. Closing it would mean loading the walk for every held market.
- **Hero.** APR = Σ payment ÷ Σ notional over that exposure × `HOURS_PER_YEAR`, labelled **APR on position notional** with that definition on hover: a funding yield on the notional held, not a return on account equity (on a leveraged account the two differ by the leverage); its text and its colour both read it at `HERO_APR_DECIMALS` (`Format.signClass`), so `0.00%` is neutral. **Hours deployed** counts distinct settlement hours held (markets held through one hour count once) over the window's hours; the All window opens an hour before its first held settlement, when the interval that settlement pays for began, and holds the settlement hours from there to the last before the snapshot time (`lastHour − (firstHeldHour − 1)`, whole hours like the fixed windows), so a position held at every settlement reads `10 / 10 h` and `100.0%` at any minute. Settlements that land in one hour count as one hour there, so Hours deployed never exceeds the window, while each still adds its notional to Σ notional and its payment to the net: a constant 1 BTC at $100,000 held through 11 settlements landing in 9 hours, paying −$10 in all, reads `9 / 168 h`, `1,100,000 dollar·hours` and an APR of −$10 ÷ $1,100,000 × 8760 = `-7.96%`. **Avg notional** is Σ notional ÷ the settlements held (`settlementsHeld`: markets held through one settlement count it once, so an hour counts as many settlements as the most any one market held in it), the account's notional per settlement held, so a constant position reads its own notional however its settlements fall in hours; **Time in market** is hours deployed ÷ window hours as a percent, never above 100% since both count the same settlement hours. **Implied hourly** is Σ payment ÷ Σ notional (see Hourly rates). **Period** is the first and last held settlement in the window, in UTC, with the year on both ends when they fall in different years. A row in the window without a size or oracle price (absent, or not wholly numeric: `1x`, `60000abc`; a size of `0` is no notional either) would put a payment in the net that the notional lacks, so APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly read `—` with that reason in the caption (`UNPRICED_PAYMENT_REASON`). A 7D / 30D / 90D window in which no position was held reads Hours deployed `0 / N h` and Time in market `0.0%`, with APR, Avg notional, Implied hourly and Period `—` and the caption "No position held in the window" (`NO_POSITION_REASON`); the All window, which then has no start, reads `—` throughout with the same caption. The caption's net is the Net Funding card's figure, the ledger's sum of each market's cents (`fundingLedger`'s `netCents` through `Format.formatCents`: `-$0.35 net on 15 dollar·hours`, never `$0` beside a non-zero APR, and two markets of +$0.005 each read `+$0.02` like the card), while the exact net stays the APR's numerator, and its dollar·hours and the Hours deployed counts group thousands like the dollar figures (`-$2,159.00 net on 215,900,000 dollar·hours`, `2,159 / 2,160 h`).
- **At cents, by the ledger rule.** The cards and the table's RECEIVED / PAID / NET show cents (`Format.formatCents`, coloured by `Format.signClass(value, Format.CENTS)`), from one ledger (`fundingLedger`): each market's NET is its exact net (Σ payments added as the exact decimals they are written as, `RiskMetrics.exactSum`) rounded once by `RiskMetrics.wholeCents`, the rule `profitLedger` rounds each market's funding by, so a market's funding reads the same cent here, in the Overview ledger and in Performance by Asset whenever `/fundingPayments` and the positions' `netFunding` agree; its RECEIVED is its exact Σ positive payments at cents, and its PAID is RECEIVED − NET, so the row foots (PAID can then differ by a cent from Σ |negative payments| rounded alone). The "N more markets" row and the cards sum the markets' cents, so the columns sum to the cards exactly as displayed.
- **Cards.** Funding Captured / Paid / Net are the ledger's received, −paid and net, each toned by `Format.signClass` of its displayed value.
- **Unparseable amounts and times.** A payment in the window whose `payment` does not parse (`RiskMetrics.exactSum`) is unknown funding, never $0, as on the Tax tab: its market's RECEIVED / PAID / NET / STATUS, the three cards and the hero's APR, Avg notional and Implied hourly read `—` with "N funding payment(s) in the window without a parseable amount" (the market's count in its cells, the window's in the cards and the hero caption); Hours deployed, Time in market and Period still show, and such a market sorts ahead of the ranked ones (an "N more markets" row holding one reads `—` too). A payment whose `createdAt` (else `effectiveAt`) does not parse is unknown funding the same way, in every window, since no window can be ruled out for it: "N funding payment(s) without a valid time" (both reasons joined with "; " when both apply); it places no held hour.
- **Table.** One row per market with funding in the window, largest |NET| first: the first `MAX_FUNDED_ROWS` on their own rows, the rest summed into one "N more markets" row, then each `ALWAYS_SHOW_TICKERS` market without funding. Rows come from the payments, not `/perpetualMarkets`, so the RECEIVED / PAID / NET columns sum to the cards. **STATUS** reads PROFIT when the NET is received, LOSS when it is paid, and EVEN when it displays as `$0.00` (a known zero, never a placeholder). **CURRENT (APR)** is the last settled hourly rate, the market's newest `/historicalFunding` row (fetched per listed market only while the tab is open; `—` with the reason while loading, on failure, with no settlement, or when that row's rate is not wholly numeric: "Last settled rate is not a number"); **PREDICTED (APR)** is `/perpetualMarkets` `nextFundingRate`, the indexer's running estimate of the next settlement (`—` with the reason for a market `/perpetualMarkets` does not list or when it did not load, for a blank rate, or for one not wholly numeric: "Predicted rate is not a number", never a hyphen or its leading digits). `defaultFundingRate1H` is a fixed per-market default parameter, not a prediction, and is not shown.
- **Missing inputs.** When `/fundingPayments` did not load (`missingDataReason(allData, 'fundingPayments', 'Funding payments')`, passed by `renderMarketTab` in `index.html`), the hero, the three cards and the table's RECEIVED / PAID / NET / STATUS read `—` with that reason (card captions, hero caption, cell titles), never `$0`; the rate columns stay. When `/fills` did not load (`inputGaps(allData).fills`), the hero reads `—` with that reason and the cards and the table still sum the payments. The same holds when a market the hero covers (one with a payment row or a held hour in the window, or a position with incomplete attribution whose window `[createdAt, closedAt]`, open-ended while OPEN, overlaps the hero's) has short fills: `processData()` passes `allData.fillGapsByMarket` (market → reason: an incomplete OPEN attribution or phantom open lots, the ledger's TRADING reasons, else the first incomplete CLOSED position's "<incompleteCause> in <market>", else an unlisted closed trade) as `gaps.fillsByMarket` and every incomplete position's window (`allData.incompleteWindows`) as `gaps.incompleteWindows`, and the hero reads `—` throughout with those markets' reasons, since the fills can no longer say which hours were held; a gap in a market the hero does not cover leaves the hero as it is. A position incomplete only for `UNKNOWN_FEE` has no short fills (its sizes, so its hours held, are right) and joins neither the map nor the windows, OPEN or CLOSED, as it joins no open-position gate. Only the hero reads the CLOSED entries: the Total Profit gate (`profitLedgerGaps`) keeps to the OPEN positions and phantom lots.

**Funding annualization.** Every funding APR (this tooltip, the funding hero, the CURRENT / PREDICTED (APR) columns) is simple-annualized over a 365-day year, `HOURS_PER_YEAR` (`DAYS_PER_FUNDING_YEAR × HOURS_PER_DAY`, 8760 h). Return, Sharpe and trade-based annualization use the Julian year, `AppConstants.MS_PER_YEAR` (365.25 days). The two are deliberately different; do not merge them.

## Metric Definitions (single source of truth)

### Per-position attribution (fills → positions)
Why per-position values come from `/fills`, where the attribution runs and the fill order it relies on: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Per-position attribution. The matching rules and output:

`RiskMetrics.attributeFillsToPositions(positions, fills)` runs one FIFO walk per market over all fills, splitting each fill into a closing and an opening portion (a reversing fill has both). The walk yields segments (net size leaves 0 … returns to 0, or reverses); a segment belongs to the position whose `createdAt` equals its first fill's, otherwise to the earliest position whose `[createdAt, closedAt]` window contains it. When several positions share that millisecond (one was opened and reversed, or closed and reopened, within a block), the segment goes to the earliest-closing one on the segment's side that has no segment yet (OPEN counts as closing last), so the chain order of segments, not the order the indexer listed the positions in, decides; same-side positions that also share `closedAt` cannot be told apart and are all marked incomplete. The walk carries lot sizes, prices and the net size as the exact decimals the fills' text is written as (integer arithmetic), so each closing step's realized is exactly `(exit − entry) × size`. Net size counts as flat within `max(1e-9, 1e-12 × the largest of the net size before the fill, the fill size and the peak |net size| since the market was last flat)` base units: decimal sizes sum to exactly 0, but a size given as a computed number carries binary residue that grows with magnitude and stays behind after the position is scaled down, and a tolerance measured only against the current fill turned that residue into a phantom reversal that made the position and every later one in the market incomplete. A reversing fill's closing portion belongs to the older position and its opening portion to the newer one. It returns `Map<position, { realized, fees, profit, peakSize, entryVwap, exitVwap, fillCount, portions, complete, incompleteCause, openedByFlip, closedByFlip, openSizeDisagrees }>`:
- `realized` — FIFO realized on the position's closing portions (gross, no fees): the exact decimal sum of the walk's exact step amounts, handed out as the Number nearest it at 15 significant digits (`NOISE_FREE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS`), so an amount below the micro is never snapped to it (`0.00499999996` reads `$0.00`)
- `fees` — `fill.fee` on its portions, a reversing fill's fee split pro-rata by size, each share the exact fraction fee × portion size ÷ fill size, never a float product (positive = paid, negative = rebate), the shares summed exactly as fractions and the sum given at 15 significant digits (`RiskMetrics.portionFeesTotal`, so a third of a fee three times is the whole fee, and shares of 0.013 and −0.007 that add to exactly 0.005 read $0.01); `null` when one of its fills has no fee (`UNKNOWN_FEE`, below)
- `profit = realized − fees` (funding excluded; it stays on the indexer's `netFunding`), the exact decimal difference at 15 significant digits, so a trade whose decimal profit is 0 (fees that exactly cancel the gain, or an exit at the average of two entries) has `profit` exactly 0 and is a scratch in every view, and a decimal tie (9.80 − 0.30 = 9.50) rounds as the decimal it is; `null` with `fees`
- `peakSize` — max |net size| during the segment, base units, always positive: the exact held size summed from the fills' decimal sizes, never the float running net size (10000 fills of 0.0003 hold exactly 3)
- `entryVwap` / `exitVwap` — size-weighted price of the opening / closing portions, Σ size × price ÷ Σ size computed exactly on the fills' decimal strings in integers (a reversing fill's two portions split its size exactly: the closing one is the exact size held before it), then taken as the Number nearest that exact quotient correctly rounded (half up) to `NOISE_FREE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` (15) significant digits, the display core's noise floor, so one rounding by the core reads the decimal the VWAP stands for (27 fills of 1.1 at 42424.42425 have an entry VWAP of exactly 42424.42425, where the float sum lies below that tie by more than the 15-digit noise floor removes); private `exactQuotientNumber`
- `fillCount` — fills touching the position (a reversing fill counts for both)
- `portions` — the position's fill portions in walk order, `{ fill, realized, fee }`: `realized` (the step's exact amount at 15 significant digits) is non-zero only on a closing portion and `fee` is the portion's size share of `fill.fee` at 15 significant digits (`null` when the fill has no fee), so they sum to `realized` and, through `RiskMetrics.portionFeesTotal(portions)` (the exact sum of the exact shares, `null` while one is unknown), to `fees`. The Tax report dates each by its fill (see Tax report, Year boundary)
- `openedByFlip` / `closedByFlip` — the position was opened / closed by a reversing fill
- `complete = false` when fills are missing, no segment (or more than one) matched, the segment's side disagrees with the position's, a CLOSED position's segment did not return to flat by its `closedAt` (a return exactly at `closedAt` counts; a later one means the closing fill is missing and the later fill may belong to a position absent from the list), same-side positions sharing both `createdAt` and `closedAt` made the match ambiguous, one of its segments starts or ends with a reversing fill that has no listed partner (a segment the walk opened by a flip needs an opposite-side position whose `closedAt` equals the segment's start; one it closed by a flip needs an opposite-side position whose `createdAt` equals the reversal moment; a missing partner means a fill is missing and the walk's net size is off; private `flipPartnersListed`), a fill the walk cannot use (unparseable `price` / `size` / `side`) lies inside the window (a fill with no parseable `createdAt` counts as inside every window in its market), one of its portions' fills has no fee (absent, blank or not wholly numeric: unknown, never `$0`; private `fillFee`, the one fee reader, a reported `'0'` a known zero; the walk still uses the fill's price, size and side, so the net size and every other position stay right), or an OPEN position's indexer `size` (signed, negative for a SHORT; OPEN rows carry a reliable size) differs from the signed net size the walk ends its market on by more than the walk's flat tolerance, or is missing (private `openSizeMatchesWalk`; a missing fill before the position can shift the walk's size without changing its side or flips), or, for a CLOSED position, its market's fills hold a closed round trip (a segment that returned to flat) whose first fill no listed position's window contains: a CLOSED row is missing, so every CLOSED position of that market is incomplete and the classifier reads `—` rather than omitting the trade. An open segment with no owner leaves positions complete (the open-lots check in Total Profit catches it). `complete` is `true` exactly when `incompleteCause` is `null`.
- `incompleteCause` — `null` when complete, otherwise the value of the frozen `RiskMetrics.INCOMPLETE_CAUSE` naming why, a phrase a panel can show as is. In precedence order (when several apply, the first wins): `UNUSABLE_FILL` 'Unusable fill', `REVERSAL_PARTNER_MISSING` 'Reversal partner missing', `INDISTINGUISHABLE` 'Indistinguishable positions', `NO_MATCHING_FILLS` 'No matching fills' (also the cause when fills are missing), `FLAT_MID_POSITION` 'Fills go flat mid-position' (more than one segment matched), `SIDE_MISMATCH` 'Fills on the opposite side', `NOT_FLAT_AT_CLOSE` 'Fills not flat at close', `OPEN_SIZE_MISMATCH` 'Fills do not match open positions', `UNKNOWN_FEE` 'Unknown fill fee', `UNLISTED_TRADE` 'Fills hold a closed trade no listed position owns' (CLOSED positions only, so it never meets the open-size case). The open-size case comes after the root causes because a skipped or missing fill usually also makes the size disagree, and the root cause is the more useful message; the unknown fee comes after every cause that makes the realized, size or prices wrong, since it leaves them right and says less; the unlisted trade is last because it is a fact about the market, and a cause on the position itself says more. The open-position gate reads it (see Total Profit, Open-position gate).
- The returned Map also carries `unlistedTradeMarkets`, the account-level report: the sorted markets whose fills hold a closed round trip no listed position owns, a market with fills but no listed position at all included (`[]` when none, or without fills). `processData()` words it as `unlistedTradeReason(markets)` in `index.html`, `UNLISTED_TRADE` followed by " in " and the markets (e.g. "Fills hold a closed trade no listed position owns in LINK-USD"), and treats it as `unlistedTradeGap`: the closed-trade list is short, so the classifier and every view built on it (see Trade classification), the hold-time histogram, the Behavior tab's Long Hold / Quick Flip counts, the entry heatmap, Most Active Day and the Positions board read `—` or show that reason.
- `openSizeDisagrees` — true for an OPEN position whose indexer `size` differs from, or cannot be compared with, the walk's final net size, whatever else made it incomplete (an unusable fill that also shifts the size leaves `incompleteCause` at 'Unusable fill' and this flag `true`): the market's fills do not add up to the open position, so the market's FIFO totals are off too. `false` for every other position, including an OPEN one incomplete only for another reason. No panel reads it; the open-position gate keys off `incompleteCause`. A position with no matched segment carries `realized = fees = profit = 0` and null size / prices.

`RiskMetrics.oppositeSide(side)` is the one side-flip rule: `'SHORT'` for `'LONG'`, `'LONG'` for `'SHORT'`, `null` for anything else (case-sensitive; callers upper-case first). The attribution's reversal-partner check and the Positions board's flip-tag tooltip both use it.

`RiskMetrics.hasCompleteAttribution(p)` is the one completeness rule: only `complete === true` counts, so a position with no attribution fields at all is incomplete. The classifier, `peakNotional` / `tradeReturn`, the Positions board, the Behavior double-down detector and the Tax report all call it. `RiskMetrics.timestampMs(iso)` is the one timestamp parser (null when absent or unparseable) the attribution and the Tax report window fills with. `RiskMetrics.holdMs(p)` is the one hold-time rule, `closedAt − createdAt` (null when either is unparseable or the close precedes the entry; a zero hold is valid), read by the Hold Time Distribution, the Behavior tab's hold-time figures and the Positions board's DURATION; `RiskMetrics.holdTimeGap(positions)` is `''` when every CLOSED position has a hold time, else e.g. "2 closed positions without a valid entry and close time".

### Trade classification
A position with `status === 'CLOSED'` is bucketed by its fill-attributed `profit` (net of fees, excluding funding):
- `> 0` → **win**
- `< 0` → **loss**
- `== 0` → **scratch**

`decisiveCount = winCount + lossCount` (excludes scratches). Use `decisiveCount` as the denominator for win-rate-style ratios so the same value never reads two ways across cards.

**All-or-nothing, account-wide.** A closed position without complete attribution (`hasCompleteAttribution`) lands in `incomplete`; while any exists, every derived field (`winRate`, `profitFactor`, `avgWin`, `avgLoss`, `expectancy`, `payoff`, `breakevenWinRate`, `bestTrade`, `worstTrade`) is `null` and `incompleteReason` reads e.g. "3 positions missing fill data". The optional `unavailableReason` argument does the same for a CLOSED list that is itself unknown or short: `processData()` passes the CLOSED list's missing-input reason (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Missing inputs), else the attribution's `unlistedTradeGap` (see Per-position attribution), so an unavailable or incomplete list reads as that reason rather than as an account with no trades or a sum over the listed ones. Consumers test `incompleteReason !== ''`. Win Rate, Profit Factor, Avg Win, Avg Loss, Payoff, Expectancy, Kelly (its caption), the Strategy Edge verdict, streaks, win-rate trend, the Win/Loss and Position Size distributions, the per-trade Sharpe fallback and the Behavior tab's profit-based cards then render `—` with that reason (an empty bucket reads `N/A` instead); the entry heatmap and Most Active Day show the reason when the CLOSED list or the OPEN rows (`openPositionsGap`) are unavailable or a position has no valid entry time (`RiskMetrics.entryTimeGap`), the Long Hold / Quick Flip rows read `—` with it, and the Post-Loss Double Down FREQUENCY reads `—` with it (see Behavior tab). The Monthly and Performance-by-Asset tables follow the account: `processData()` passes the account classifier's `incompleteReason` to `renderTables` (`gaps.classifier`), which hands it as `unavailableReason` to `classifyByMonth(positions, unavailableReason)` and to each per-asset `classifyClosed`, so one incomplete position anywhere blanks every month's and every asset's ratios (and the per-asset Sharpe) together with the header cards. `classifyByMonth` buckets by `closedAt` alone (`RiskMetrics.monthKeyUTC`), never by `createdAt`: while a CLOSED position has no valid `closedAt` its month is unknown, so it applies `RiskMetrics.closeTimeGap(positions)` ("1 closed position without a valid close time", the wording `holdTimeGap` shares) to every month, and every month's WIN RATE, TRADES, AVG WIN, AVG LOSS and PROFIT FACTOR read `—` with it; the asset rows, whose market is known, keep their values. Their TRADES cell reads `decisive (+scratches)`, the plain closed count with the reason on hover while the account holds an incomplete position, or `—` with the reason when the CLOSED list is unavailable (or, in the Monthly table, a close time is unknown); every classifier cell that reads `—` for an incomplete input carries the reason on hover.

**Empty buckets.** With complete inputs, a figure whose bucket is empty reads `N/A`: Payoff (both cards, each caption naming the bucket through `RiskMetrics.payoffEmptyBucketReason(cls)`: "No losses recorded", "No wins recorded" or "No decisive trades"), the Strategy Edge panel's win rate, profit factor and implied payoff, the Time Analysis hold times and best / worst hour (no entry hour with `TUNABLES.HOUR_MIN_SAMPLE` trades; all four read `N/A` with "No closed positions" for an account without a closed position), and a pattern row's SUCCESS RATE / EXPECTANCY with no closed win or loss (only scratches, or only open double-down re-entries); the reason is the cell's tooltip. While the classifier's inputs are incomplete the same cells read `—` with `incompleteReason` as the tooltip.

**Streaks, trend and verdict.** Closing order is one comparator, `RiskMetrics.byClosingOrder(fills)`: `closedAt` (an unparseable one last), then closes in one millisecond by their closing fills' chain order (`fillChainIndex`, the order of the trade-path drawdown series), so neither the indexer's listing order nor the win / loss split moves a card; Max Consec. Wins / Losses, Win Rate Trend and the per-trade ratios (`decisiveByCloseTime`) sort by it (`processData()` passes the fills to `renderMetrics`, `renderTables` and `computeTradeBasedMetrics`). Max Consec. Wins / Losses count the longest run of wins / losses in closing order; the caption is the summed profit of that run, added exactly (`RiskMetrics.exactSum`), and of equally long runs the one with the larger summed |profit|; it is toned by the $ it shows (`Format.signClass`, so a run that reads `$0` is neutral), and a reason caption carries no tone. Scratches are skipped: they neither extend nor break a run. Win Rate Trend compares the win rate of the last N decisive trades with the all-time rate, where N is `TUNABLES.RECENT_DECISIVE_CAP` or half the decisive trades, whichever is smaller, so the window is never the whole history; its arrow and tone (↑ green / ↓ red beyond `TREND_TONE_POINTS`, 1 point, else → neutral) compare the two rates as displayed (`Format.asDisplayed` at `Format.PERCENT_DECIMALS`), so the same `60.0% vs 59.0%` always gets the same arrow; with fewer than 4 decisive trades it reads `—` with "Needs ≥4 decisive trades". The Strategy Edge verdict between the win-rate archetypes (40% ≤ WR < 60%, or any win rate whose profit factor fits no archetype) reads by the profit factor as displayed: "Profitable…" only above 1.00, break-even ("Break-even: gross wins and gross losses cancel…") at exactly 1.00, and a negative edge, never a marginal one, below it (`BREAK_EVEN_PROFIT_FACTOR`, private to `src/panels/overview.js`). The verdict classifies the win rate and profit factor as the readout displays them (the win rate through `Format.asDisplayed` at `Format.PERCENT_DECIMALS`, the rounding of `Format.formatPercent`; the profit factor through `Format.asDisplayed` at `Format.RATIO_DECIMALS`, the rounding of `Format.fmtRatio`), so `60.0%` is never placed below the 60% line nor `1.00` below one. **One profit-factor text:** the Profit Factor card, the Strategy Edge readout and the Monthly PROFIT FACTOR column all render through `Format.fmtRatio`, so $1,990 / $2,000 reads `1.00` and $2,230 / $2,000 `1.12` everywhere. Most Active Day (UTC entry weekday) counts every position's entry, OPEN included (the heatmap's population), lists every day tied for the most entries, joined with " / ", with the count on hover, and reads `—` with the heatmap's reason (`entriesGap`: `closedTradesGap || openPositionsGap || RiskMetrics.entryTimeGap(positions)`) while an entry is unknown.

Helper: `RiskMetrics.classifyClosed(positions, unavailableReason = '')` returns `{ wins, losses, scratches, incomplete, all, grossWin, grossLoss, totalProfit, winCount, lossCount, scratchCount, decisiveCount, closedCount, incompleteCount, incompleteReason, winRate, profitFactor, avgWin, avgLoss, expectancy, payoff, breakevenWinRate, bestTrade, worstTrade }`. `totalProfit = grossWin − grossLoss` (fill-attributed, net of fees); `grossWin`, `grossLoss` and `totalProfit` are exact sums of the profits (`exactSum`), and `avgWin`, `avgLoss`, `expectancy`, `profitFactor` and `payoff = avgWin / avgLoss` are exact quotients of those sums handed on as the Number nearest the quotient at 15 significant digits, so the display rounds the decimal a mean stands for once (19 wins summing to `$175,341.50` read AVG WIN `+$9,229`; ten trades summing to `$5.00` read EXPECTANCY `+$1`) and every reader (the Overview cards, Monthly AVG WIN / AVG LOSS, the Behavior EXPECTANCY, SUCCESS RATE tone and READING, Payoff, Kelly) gets that one value; `breakevenWinRate = 100 / (1 + payoff)`, the win rate (percent) at which expectancy is zero; `bestTrade` is the single largest winning `profit` and `worstTrade` the single most negative losing `profit` (kept negative), each `null` without a win / loss and, like every derived field, while `incompleteReason` is set. The Payoff cards, the Strategy Edge's implied payoff and Kelly (`winRate / 100 − (1 − winRate / 100) / payoff`) read these fields rather than re-deriving them (a Kelly that displays below zero, `Format.asDisplayed` at `Format.PERCENT_DECIMALS`, is captioned "Negative edge: Kelly fraction 0" in place of the markup's "Kelly fraction (theoretical)", which returns otherwise); the two Payoff captions take their "WR x% vs y% breakeven" text and tone from `Format.breakevenVerdict(winRate, breakevenWinRate, expectancy)` (both rates through `Format.formatPercent`; compared as displayed, profit tone above the breakeven rate and loss tone below it; when the two display alike the tone is the expectancy's at whole dollars, `Format.signClass(expectancy)`, neutral at `$0`, so `25.0% vs 25.0%` beside a `-$4` expectancy reads as a loss; this one tie rule also tones the Behavior SUCCESS RATE). **One win-rate text:** every win rate (the Win Rate card, the Strategy Edge readout, the Payoff captions and the "needed to break even" caption, Win Rate Trend and its all-time rate, the Monthly and Performance-by-Asset WIN RATE, the Behavior patterns' SUCCESS RATE) renders through `Format.formatPercent`, so the same rate never reads two ways (3 wins of 2000 decisive trades is `0.2%` everywhere). Avg Win, Avg Loss and Expectancy cells take their colour from `Format.signClass`, so a negative expectancy reads as a loss. Performance by Asset shows AVG PROFIT (`expectancy`, rounded once from the exact quotient by `RiskMetrics.quotientCents`), BEST and WORST (rounded through `RiskMetrics.wholeCents` like the row's PROFIT) at cents (see Exact sums, one rounding). The percent unit is `AppConstants.PERCENT`.

### Per-trade return
Two labelled per-trade returns, for two different questions:

- **Return on peak notional** (how well the trade did for its size): `r = profit / peakNotional`, where `peakNotional = peakSize × entryVwap` (the exact product of the decimals they stand for, and the return their exact quotient, each handed on at 15 significant digits, so 3.015 on 3 BTC at $100 reads `+1.01%`) — profit as a fraction of the peak notional held during the position, all from `attributeFillsToPositions`. Peak notional, not cumulative entered size, which overstates capital on scaled positions. Used by the Win/Loss Distribution and the Positions board's PROFIT % so they never disagree. Helpers: `RiskMetrics.peakNotional(p)` (null when the attribution is incomplete or either factor is not positive; also the Position Size Distribution's size and the Behavior double-down detector's size) and `RiskMetrics.tradeReturn(p)` (null when the attribution is incomplete or the notional is undefined).
- **Return on equity at open** (what the trade did to the account): `r = profit / base`, where `base` is the Modified Dietz base of the `/historical-pnl` period holding the position's `createdAt`, the same base the time-weighted returns use (`periodReturnBase`, private to `risk-metrics.js`): the equity of the row at or before the open (0 when the open precedes every row) plus `max(0, netTransfers)` of the row after it. A deposit credited between the hourly row and the open (the usual way an empty account is funded before its first trade) is therefore in the base; because `/transfers` is not fetched, an inflow later in the same period is too. Every per-trade Sharpe / Sortino / Calmar (the Performance-by-Asset SHARPE column and the trade-based fallback cards) is built on it. Equity at open gives each trade's return the sign of that trade's own profit (on notional, a small-notional winner would weigh as much as a large-notional loser); it does not make a ratio's sign, or the compounded return's, agree with the dollar result of several trades: each return divides by a different base, so a win on a small account can outweigh larger dollar losses on a bigger one. Every per-row SHARPE cell therefore carries its compounded return and that caveat (see Time-weighted returns, Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar). Helper: `RiskMetrics.tradeReturnsOnEquity(positions, historicalPnl)` → one return per position, null when the attribution is incomplete or that base is not positive or is dust, below the account-wide floor the time-weighted returns apply (`minReturnBase`, private to `risk-metrics.js`: `TWR_MIN_BASE_SHARE_OF_MEDIAN_EQUITY` of the median positive equity of the rows) ("No funded equity at open"), so one trade opened on a near-empty account cannot dominate a per-trade ratio.

### Total Profit (headline)
`totalPnL = FIFO realized over /fills + FIFO unrealized of the lots left open + Σ netFunding over every position − Σ fees over every fill`. Realized is computed **bottom-up from /fills via FIFO inventory walk** (`computeRealizedFromFills`), NOT from `/perpetualPositions.realizedPnl` (why: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Per-position attribution). FIFO over the raw fill records reconciles to the equity-based curve within float-rounding. The Total Profit hero card surfaces the split as a three-cell ledger:

- **TRADING** = `computeRealizedFromFills(fills).total + computeUnrealizedFromFills(fills, marketsMap).total`. Unrealized is the lots the same FIFO walk leaves open, marked at `marketsMap[market].oraclePrice`, NOT the indexer's per-position `unrealizedPnl`: that field marks against the average entry of every opening fill, a different cost basis from FIFO realized, so adding the two double-counts profit FIFO has already realized on a partly reduced position, a material headline error on any account that scales out of positions.
- **FUNDING** = Σ netFunding over every position — positive contribution when received, negative when paid
- **FEES** = `−feesTotal(fills)` — displayed as a *signed contribution to profit*. dYdX `fill.fee` is positive when the user paid (taker / most maker fills) and negative for maker rebates, so the cell renders the paid amount as a negative dollar value (red) and rebates as positive (green)

Headline math: `TRADING + FUNDING + FEES_contribution`.

**Exact sums, one rounding.** Indexer amounts are decimal strings. Funding (Σ `netFunding`, Σ payments) and fees (Σ `fill.fee`) are added as the exact decimals they are written as (`RiskMetrics.exactSum`: decimal text digit by digit, a computed number at its 15-significant-digit decimal; the sum handed out as the Number nearest it at 15 significant digits), so a sum carries no binary-float drift, and every amount shown at cents rounds once through `RiskMetrics.wholeCents`: from the decimal the amount stands for (decimal text digit by digit; a computed number at its noise-free decimal, 15 significant digits, the display core's rule, never snapped to the micro first, so a FIFO unrealized `0.00499999996` reads `$0.00`), half away from zero on both signs, so a decimal half-cent tie rounds as the decimal it is (`1.005` → `1.01`, `-0.125` → `-0.13`), never by the float just below it. The FIFO walk carries lot sizes and prices as exact decimals, so each step's realized (`(exit − entry) × size`, signed) and each open lot's unrealized (`(oracle − entry) × size`) are exact decimals and every sum of them is exact (`computeRealizedFromFills`, `computeUnrealizedFromFills`, the attribution's portions); each figure is handed out as the Number nearest that decimal at 15 significant digits, so a price difference whose binary cancellation leaves more noise than 15 digits drop (`64000.13 − 64000.1`, as realized or against the oracle) still reads its decimal (a FIFO `0.015` → `0.02`) and an amount below the micro is never snapped to it (a realized `0.00499999996` reads `$0.00` in TRADING, the headline and the Tax row), and `profitLedger` adds a market's realized and unrealized as the exact decimals they stand for before its one rounding. `profitLedger`, the Market tab's funding ledger, the Tax report and Performance by Asset's BEST / WORST all round through it. The per-position attribution's `realized`, `fees` and `profit` and the Monthly PROFIT `delta` are exact decimal sums too. A quotient (the Tax report's EUR = USD ÷ quote, Performance by Asset's AVG PROFIT = the decisive trades' exact profit sum ÷ their count) rounds to cents once from the exact quotient (`RiskMetrics.quotientCents`), not from a float quotient taken first to the micro, so a mean of `0.0049995` reads `$0.00`.

**One display rounding.** Every number on screen (currency at whole dollars and at cents, percents, ratios including Profit Factor and payoff, Taker Share, funding APR, win rates, the counts and spans in captions and tooltips) rounds through the one rule in `src/format.js` (`_displayedMagnitude`): half away from zero on the decimal the value stands for, taken at 15 significant digits (`AppConstants.NOISE_FREE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS`, the noise floor `RiskMetrics` reads too) so the binary noise of the arithmetic that made it is gone (`1.005` → `1.01`, `0.15%` → `0.2%`, `1.115` → `1.12`, a funding APR of `0.00000875 × 8760` → `7.67%`) and a value just below a tie still rounds down. A displayed decimal is never rounded with a raw `toFixed`: plain numbers (caption and tooltip spans, coverage, periods per year, the Tax flat rate, the Tax ENTRY / EXIT prices and export money cells) go through `Format.fmtFixed(value, digits)`, as do the leverage cells, the funding chart's rate ticks, the Strategy Edge axis ticks, the funding hero's hour and dollar·hour counts and the Quick Flip label; periods per year read at `Format.PERIODS_PER_YEAR_DECIMALS` in both the Sharpe / Sortino and the VaR tooltips. An hourly funding rate between steps rounds through the same rule (`0.00001015%` → `+0.0000102%`). The masthead's "Updated … ago" is `Format.formatDataAge(ms)`, which rounds each unit once from the milliseconds (89.6 minutes reads `1h`, never `2h` through a rounded 90 minutes). Every span (`Format.formatDuration`: the Behavior hold times, the Positions DURATION, the Drawdown Periods DURATION / TO TROUGH / RECOVERY and Current Drawdown's time below peak, which all share it) rounds its smallest unit shown once from the milliseconds through `asDisplayed` and carries into the larger unit, keeping the unit by magnitude (`7d 17.7h` → `7d 18h`, 59m 59.9s → `1h 0m`, 23h 59.7m → `1d 0h`); `Format.displayedDurationMs(ms)` is that displayed span in milliseconds (whole minutes below a day, whole hours from one), which `formatDuration` prints and every hold-time bucket (the Hold Time Distribution, Long Hold, Quick Flip) bins on, so a trade lands where its DURATION reads. A size (`Format.fmtAssetSize`) rounds at `AppConstants.SIZE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` by the same rule as a price (`0.1234567890125` → `0.123456789013`), grouped the same way. A price (`Format.formatPrice`) rounds at its significant digits by the same rule, with a carry across a power of ten (`999999.5` → `$1,000,000`), so a float VWAP just below a decimal tie (64012.25 stored as 64012.24999…) reads `$64,012.3`, as the Tax tab's 4-decimal prices round their ties (`TaxReport.priceText`). A verdict, tier, tone or arrow is decided on `Format.asDisplayed(value, digits)`, the number the cell shows, so classification always follows the digits on screen; a comparison of two computed amounts that no cell rounds (the Behavior double-down size test) is made on `Format.asDecimal(value)`, the decimal the value stands for at the same 15 significant digits. The funding chart's Annualized tooltip and the CURRENT / PREDICTED (APR) cells annualize through the one `Format.fundingAprPercent`, `null` for a rate that is not wholly numeric.

**At cents, summed from the markets.** The ledger, the headline and the Performance-by-Asset money columns show cents with thousands separators (`Format.formatCents`: `-$18,452.63`, `$0.00`; coloured by `Format.signClass(value, Format.CENTS)`), and add up exactly as displayed: `RiskMetrics.profitLedger(marketPnL(...))` rounds each market's trading (realized + unrealized), funding and fees to whole cents first, makes the market's PROFIT `trading + funding − fees` of those cents, and sums the markets' cents into TRADING, FUNDING, FEES and the headline. `processData()` reads the four totals from it and passes its `byMarket` (marketPnL's slots at cents) to Performance by Asset and the Overview chart, so the asset rows add up to the ledger and the ledger to the headline; rounding each total from the raw sum instead can differ from its displayed rows by a cent per market.

**Missing inputs.** A component whose inputs did not load (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Missing inputs) renders `—`: TRADING needs `/fills` and an oracle price for every market with open lots (else the markets endpoint's missing-input reason or "No oracle price for …"), FUNDING needs the OPEN rows (`openPositionsGap`), the CLOSED list and a `netFunding` on every position, FEES needs `/fills` and a fee on every fill: a fill whose `fee` is absent, blank or not wholly numeric has an unknown fee, never `$0`, and `RiskMetrics.feeGap(fills)` words it ("N fill(s) without a fee"). A position's `netFunding` that is absent, blank or not wholly numeric is unknown, never `$0` (`RiskMetrics.positionNetFunding(p)`, the one reader; a reported `'0'` is a known zero), and `RiskMetrics.netFundingGap(positions)` words it: "No netFunding reported for N position(s)" (`RiskMetrics.NO_NET_FUNDING`). Fees, `netFunding`, equity and the Market tab's numbers (payment sizes and oracle prices, settlement prices and rates, `nextFundingRate`, candle closes) are read through `RiskMetrics.decimalNumberOf(value)`: a number, or `null` when the value is absent, blank or not wholly numeric (`'60000abc'`), never its leading digits. Any `—` component blanks the headline too, with the reason as its caption, and skips the reconciliation guard. `processData()` derives these from the per-input reasons as `profitLedgerGaps(allData, inputs, openPositionsGap, unpricedMarkets, fillGapsByMarket, positions, fills)` in `index.html` and passes them on so Performance by Asset and the Overview chart tooltip blank the same components. `unpricedMarkets` is `computeUnrealizedFromFills(...).unpricedMarkets`; `unpricedReason(data, markets)` words their gap: '' for none, else the markets endpoint's missing-input reason or "No oracle price for A, B".

**Open-position gate.** Fills that loaded can still be short. `fillGapsByMarket` maps each affected market to one reason:
- any OPEN position whose attribution is incomplete (`RiskMetrics.hasCompleteAttribution` false) for a cause other than `UNKNOWN_FEE` (an unknown fee leaves the market's FIFO totals right; FEES carries it): `incompleteOpenPositionReason(p)` gives "<incompleteCause> in <market>" (e.g. "Unusable fill in BTC-USD", "Reversal partner missing in BTC-USD"), except the open-size cause, which reads "Fills do not match open positions" alone. The first such position in a market names that market's reason;
- while the OPEN rows are known, a market whose fills end with open lots (`computeUnrealizedFromFills(...).openMarkets`) but lists no OPEN row (a closing fill is missing, so the lots are phantom): "Fills do not match open positions".

That wording is `OPEN_SIZE_MISMATCH_REASON` in `index.html`, taken from `RiskMetrics.INCOMPLETE_CAUSE.OPEN_SIZE_MISMATCH` so it is defined once. TRADING reads `—` with the fills reason when `/fills` failed, otherwise with the unpriced reason followed by each distinct `fillGapsByMarket` reason, joined with "; "; the headline takes the first gap of TRADING, FUNDING, FEES as its caption. Per-market profit is scoped to its market: `profitLedgerGaps(...).marketProfit(market)` is the fills reason, else that market's `fillGapsByMarket` reason, else that market's own unpriced reason (`unpricedReason(data, [market])`), else that market's funding gap, `marketFunding(market)`: the OPEN-rows or CLOSED-list reason, else `netFundingGap` over that market's positions, else that market's fee gap, `marketFees(market)`: the fills reason, else `feeGap` over that market's fills. A gap in one market blanks only that market's Performance-by-Asset PROFIT (and, for funding, its FUNDING; for fees, its FEES) and Overview chart tooltip profit; other markets stay numeric, and FEES stays numeric unless a fee is unknown.

Helpers:
- `RiskMetrics.computeRealizedFromFills(fills)` — `{ total, byMarket }` from FIFO inventory walk, each step's exact realized summed exactly on the decimals; handles position flips atomically (closes current lots, opens fresh opposite-side inventory at the flip-fill price for residual size)
- `RiskMetrics.profitLedger(marketAgg)` — `{ byMarket, trading, funding, fees, total }` at cents (above); a market's `netFunding` / `total`, and the account's `funding` / `total`, are `null` while that funding is unknown; its `fees` / `total`, and the account's, likewise while those fees are
- `RiskMetrics.netFundingTotal(positions)` — Σ netFunding at full precision, `null` while any position's is unknown; `RiskMetrics.netFundingGap(positions)` — why ('' when known)
- `RiskMetrics.feesTotal(fills)` — sum of `fill.fee` (positive = paid), `null` while any fill's fee is unknown; `RiskMetrics.feeGap(fills)` — why ('' when known)
- `RiskMetrics.marketFees(fills)` — `{ [market]: feesPaid }` map for the per-asset table, `null` for a market holding a fill without a fee (`marketPnL` then leaves that market's `fees` and `total` `null`)
- `RiskMetrics.computeUnrealizedFromFills(fills, marketsMap)` — `{ total, byMarket, unpricedMarkets, openMarkets }`; `openMarkets` lists every market whose walk ends with open lots; a flat market contributes 0, a market with open lots and no positive oracle price is `null` in `byMarket` and listed in `unpricedMarkets`, and `total` is then `null`. Realized + unrealized equals the fills' cash flow plus the marked inventory.
- `RiskMetrics.equityAdjustedTotalPnl(historicalPnl, equityNow)` — the guard's reference (below)

A reconciliation guard fires (`console.error`) when `|headline − reference| > max($1, |reference|×1%)`, where the reference is `equityAdjustedTotalPnl`: the latest `/historical-pnl` `totalPnl` moved by the subaccount's equity change since that row (the rows are hourly, so the raw latest row lags the live headline by the price drift since it; the adjustment assumes no transfer since the row). On an account with complete `/fills` to inception the headline and the reference agree within float rounding, so the guard is expected to stay quiet there, but an equity flow the indexer does not expose per event (rewards, insurance fund, ADL) can still open a gap of a few percent; if it fires, the structured payload (trading / funding / fees / equityBased / gap / row counts / activeChildSubaccounts) tells the operator which stream to inspect.

Classification (`classifyClosed`) buckets by the fill-attributed per-position `profit` (see Per-position attribution), cut from the same per-market FIFO walk as `computeRealizedFromFills`; the classifier's figures are net of fees, the headline additionally carries funding and unrealized.

### Per-market Profit
`total = realized (FIFO from /fills) + FIFO unrealized of that market's open lots + netFunding across every position in that market − fees on fills in that market`. Used by the Overview chart tooltip AND the Performance-by-Asset table, which follow the headline's missing-input rule (PROFIT / FUNDING / FEES read `—` when their inputs did not load; PROFIT also reads `—` for a market under the open-position gate or with open lots and no oracle price, with that market's reason). While `openPositionsGap` is set, the table lists every market rather than hiding one with no known position; it also lists a market without positions whose fills add to the ledger (non-zero PROFIT or FEES, e.g. an unlisted trade), so its rows always add up to the ledger. Helper: `RiskMetrics.marketPnL(positions, feesMap?, realizedByMarket?, unrealizedByMarket?)` (returns `{ realizedClosed, unrealizedOpen, netFunding, fees, total, closedCount, openCount }`; `netFunding` and `total` are `null` while any of the market's positions has no `netFunding`), shown at cents through `profitLedger` (see Total Profit).

- `feesMap` is optional `{ [market]: feesPaid }` (positive = paid). Omitting it leaves `fees` at 0.
- `realizedByMarket` is optional `{ [market]: realized }` from `computeRealizedFromFills(...).byMarket`. When provided, it OVERRIDES the sum of the per-position indexer `realizedPnl` field as the source for `slot.realizedClosed`. Omitting it falls back to that indexer sum (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Per-position attribution, says why production never does).
- `unrealizedByMarket` is optional `{ [market]: unrealized }` from `computeUnrealizedFromFills(...).byMarket`. When provided, it OVERRIDES the sum of the indexer `unrealizedPnl` of OPEN positions as `slot.unrealizedOpen` (a `null` entry adds 0; the caller blanks PROFIT through the TRADING gap).

Every money column of the Performance-by-Asset table (PROFIT, FUNDING, FEES, AVG PROFIT, BEST, WORST) renders at cents (`Format.formatCents`). The AVG PROFIT column is the per-asset classifier's `expectancy`, at cents as `RiskMetrics.quotientCents(exactSum(decisive profits), decisiveCount)`. BEST / WORST are single trades, not averages: the per-asset `classifyClosed` result's `bestTrade` / `worstTrade` (fill-attributed, net of fees, excluding funding; see Trade classification). WORST renders as a negative dollar figure. Each reads a bare `—` when the asset has no trade of that kind, and AVG PROFIT, BEST and WORST read `—` with the classifier's `incompleteReason` on hover while it is set (all-or-nothing, see Trade classification), so an unknown figure never looks like an empty bucket.

### Monthly Performance Breakdown — PNL column
Months are UTC calendar months, like every other date view: `RiskMetrics.monthKeyUTC(timestamp)` gives the one month key (`'YYYY-MM'`, null for an unparseable timestamp) that `classifyByMonth`, `histPnlMonthly` and the table's per-month `/historical-pnl` slices all bucket by, so the table does not change with the viewer's timezone. Rows sort newest first on that key as a string (never by re-parsing the display label, which Safari cannot), and `RiskMetrics.monthLabel(key)` renders it (`September 2026`); the header reads MONTH (UTC).

`PNL = lastOfMonth.totalPnl − lastOfPriorMonth.totalPnl` from `/historical-pnl`, where the current month's last value is the live point (see Headline Max Drawdown). Each cell shows the month-end and the prior month-end (the inception 0 for the first month) each at whole dollars (`Format.asDisplayed(v, Format.WHOLE_DOLLARS)`), differenced, and takes its tone from that displayed difference, so the cells telescope: the column sums exactly to the Total Profit headline rounded to whole dollars (the card itself shows cents; month-ends `+$0.50`, `+$1.00` and a live `+$1.50` read `+$1`, `$0`, `+$1`, summing to `+$2`), where each month's own change rounded alone could drift by up to half a dollar a month. Same series as headline drawdown and MAX DD on the row, so the column is dimensionally consistent with every other monthly column; the current month's MAX DD also runs to the live point, its SHARPE stays on the rows' returns. Helper: `RiskMetrics.histPnlMonthly(historicalPnl, live)` returns `{ [monthKey]: { delta, start, end, hasData, reason } }` (`start` / `end` the prior month-end and month-end it differences, `delta` their exact decimal difference; all three null while the change is unknown) for every calendar month from the first row's to the last row's. A month with no `/historical-pnl` rows reads `hasData: false`, `delta: null`, and so does the following month's `delta` (its prior month-end is unknown), rather than that month absorbing the gap month's change; both render `—` with `reason` on hover (no-metric > wrong-metric). A month outside the rows' span (only trades) renders `—` with "No /historical-pnl rows this month". The earliest observed month gets `delta = firstRow.totalPnl − 0`: `/historical-pnl` is walked to inception, and the anchored series opens at that 0 (see Headline Max Drawdown). Rows that do not reach inception (`historyCut`, see Headline Max Drawdown) get no 0: the month they start in is cut short, so its PROFIT, MAX DD and SHARPE read `—` with the cut reason (`histPnlMonthly` / `histPnlMonthlyDrawdown(historicalPnl, live, historyCut)`; `renderTables` gets it as `gaps.historyCut`), as do those of every earlier month and the Performance-by-Asset SHARPE of a market with a trade opened before the first kept row; the later months keep their values. When `/historical-pnl` failed to load, every monthly PROFIT, MAX DD and SHARPE and the Performance-by-Asset SHARPE give that failure (`missingDataReason`, `gaps.historicalPnl`) as their reason ahead of the empty-data wording ("No /historical-pnl rows this month", "No funded equity at open"). The PROFIT header's tooltip states this definition: the current month ends at the live Total Profit, each month-end is taken at whole dollars so the column adds up to the Total Profit headline rounded to whole dollars, the first month runs from 0 at inception, `—` for a month without rows, the month after one, or a month a cached snapshot starts partway through, and only WIN RATE through PROFIT FACTOR come from the month's closed trades (MAX DD and SHARPE come from the `/historical-pnl` series).

MAX DD runs on the same anchored series from the same base as PROFIT: `RiskMetrics.histPnlMonthlyDrawdown(historicalPnl, live)` → `{ [monthKey]: { dollarDrawdown, peakValue, troughValue, reason } }` is the deepest peak-to-trough as displayed (its `peakValue` and `troughValue`, `dollarDrawdown` their raw difference; all three null with `reason` when unknown), and the cell shows `Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peakValue, troughValue)` (see Headline Max Drawdown, One displayed drawdown), from the prior month-end point (when the prior calendar month has points) through the month's own points, the live point in its month, so a drop at the month boundary counts in both columns. Both helpers read one private bucketing of the series by month (`seriesByMonth`). It needs `MIN_DRAWDOWN_POINTS` (2) points, so one row plus the prior month-end, or one row plus the live point, is enough. A month that never drew down, or whose drawdown displays as `$0`, reads `$0` with the zero class; `—` only with its reason on hover (no rows this month, or one point and no prior month-end). Win Rate / Avg Win / Avg Loss / Profit Factor in the same row come from `RiskMetrics.classifyByMonth` (fill-attributed `profit`, bucketed by `closedAt`); while any closed position in the account is incomplete or has no valid close time, or the CLOSED list is unavailable, every month renders them `—` with the reason (see Trade classification).

### Headline Max Drawdown
$ DD on cumulative `totalPnl` from `/historical-pnl` (realized + unrealized profit over time, excluding net transfers). Peak-to-trough in chronological order. This series captures unrealized peaks the closed-trade ledger cannot see (e.g. a large open profit that was later given back). Helper: `RiskMetrics.histPnlDrawdown(historicalPnl, live)`.

**Live anchor.** The rows are hourly (with gaps of up to days), so the series ends at a live point: `RiskMetrics.livePnlPoint(historicalPnl, headlineProfit, nowMs)` → `{ t, c }`, where `c` is the dashboard's own Total Profit headline (`profitLedger(...).total`, from fills, funding and fees) and `t` is when the data was read (`processData(snapshotAtMs)`: the cache's `fetchedAt` on hydrate, else the render time). It never reads equity, so a deposit or withdrawal after the last hourly row is never counted as profit (the dashboard does not fetch `/transfers`). It is `null` only when the headline is unavailable (`profitGap`) or there are no rows; the series then ends at its latest row and the Current Drawdown caption says so. When `nowMs` is not after the latest row (a client clock trailing the indexer's), the point is dated just after it (`LIVE_POINT_MIN_LEAD_MS`), so the series ends at the headline whenever the headline is available.

**Inception origin.** `/historical-pnl` is walked to inception (a failed page rejects the walk), and `totalPnl` is 0 before the first deposit, so when the first row already holds a profit or loss the series opens with `INCEPTION_TOTAL_PNL` (0) dated at that row. A loss already present in the first hourly row is then measured from 0 by every drawdown view, the base `histPnlMonthly` measures the first month from, and the cumulative chart's first candle opens at 0. The trade-system series follows the same rule (`withInceptionPoint`, private to `risk-metrics.js`): cumulative closed-trade profit opens at 0 dated at the first close, so a losing first trade is a drawdown of its loss on the fallback path too, as the same profit would be on `/historical-pnl`. The time-weighted returns open with the same inception period (see Time-weighted returns).

**Rows cut by the cache.** A cached snapshot whose `/historical-pnl` the cache trimmed to its newest rows (`PortfolioCache.evictionOf(data).trimmed`) does not reach inception. `processData()` derives one reason for it, `historyCutReason` in `index.html` ("Historical profit before YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC not in cached snapshot"), and passes it as `historyCut` to `buildCumulativeTotalPnlSeries(historicalPnl, live, historyCut)` and every reader of that series, which then opens at its first row with no inception value. The all-time drawdown views cannot be measured from cut rows, so on the historical-pnl path the reason is the drawdown gap: Max Drawdown, Current Drawdown and Recovery Factor read `—` and the Drawdown Periods table holds only the reason. The all-time time-series ratios cannot be measured from cut rows either: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, VaR and Expected Shortfall read `—` with the reason (the Sharpe / Sortino and Calmar captions give it as their text and tooltip, VaR / ES as the value's tooltip; no per-trade fallback, which would need equity at opens before the first kept row). The month the rows start in reads `—` in the Monthly table (see Monthly Performance Breakdown); the chart draws the rows it has.

**Unreadable rows.** A `/historical-pnl` row whose `createdAt` does not parse (`RiskMetrics.timestampMs`), whose `totalPnl` is not wholly numeric (`''`, `'12abc'`, absent), or whose `equity` or `netTransfers` is absent, blank or not wholly numeric (the time-weighted returns and the per-trade base at open read both; the indexer always sends them), is unknown, never dropped or read as $0: `RiskMetrics.historicalPnlRowGap(rows)` words it ("1 /historical-pnl row without a valid time or amount"), and while it is set `buildCumulativeTotalPnlSeries` (its `live` point included), the time-weighted returns, `varSampleReturns` and `equityAdjustedTotalPnl` return nothing, `tradeReturnsOnEquity` returns no return and `perTradeRatios` reads `—` with the reason, so no row is ever sorted on an unparsed time (every builder, and the Monthly and Performance-by-Asset tables, read the rows through `RiskMetrics.chronologicalHistoricalPnl`, sorted on the parsed milliseconds, so a timestamp written at another UTC offset takes its place in time). `processData()` treats it like `historyCut`, but for every month: `drawdownSource` stays `'hist'` (there are rows) and the reason is the drawdown gap (Max / Current Drawdown, Recovery Factor, Drawdown Periods), the cumulative chart's empty-state reason, `gaps.historicalPnl` of `renderTables` (every monthly PROFIT, MAX DD and SHARPE and every asset SHARPE), and the Sharpe / Sortino / Calmar / VaR / Expected Shortfall reason, with no per-trade fallback.

**Plateaus.** A peak held flat over several rows is dated at its last row (where the time under water starts) in every view: the worst-event scan, the event list and Current Drawdown all move their peak to every point that regains it (`RiskMetrics.regainsPeak`: its gap to the peak displays as `$0`, so a point at or above the peak always does).

**One displayed drawdown.** Every drawdown figure on screen is its displayed peak less its displayed trough at whole dollars, `Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peak, trough)`, and every test of whether there is a drawdown reads that same value (`Format.displaysAsLoss`): the Max Drawdown card (it foots with its "Peak → trough" caption: peak `+$12,345` and trough `+$2,346` read `-$9,999`, never the raw `9,999.8` rounded alone to `-$10,000`), Current Drawdown (the displayed peak less the displayed current value), each Drawdown Periods row's DEPTH (it foots with its PEAK and TROUGH cells) and where it recovers (the displayed peak less the displayed point, `RiskMetrics.regainsPeak`), each month's MAX DD (its deepest drawdown's peak and trough), the chart's "Below peak:" line (the displayed running peak less the displayed point) and the Recovery Factor denominator (the displayed Max Drawdown). Where several drawdowns compete, the deepest is chosen by that displayed depth, the raw depth breaking ties (`RiskMetrics.deeperDrawdownFirst` over the events' `shownDepth`, which `scanDrawdownEvents` sets through `Format.drawdownAsDisplayed`, resolved at call time): the Max Drawdown card's event, on either path, is therefore always the Drawdown Periods table's top row, a month's MAX DD is its deepest event as displayed, and a candle's "Below peak:" is its deepest drop as displayed. So one drawdown with the same peak and trough reads the same everywhere, and Current Drawdown never exceeds Max Drawdown (peak `+$100.40`, now the trough `+$50.60` read `-$49` on both cards and the ongoing row, never the raw `49.8` rounded to `-$50`). A drawdown that displays as `$0` (a drop whose peak and trough display alike) is none: Max Drawdown reads `$0` with "No drawdown recorded", Current Drawdown reads at peak, Recovery Factor reads `N/A`, the Drawdown Periods table lists no such event (a drawdown ends where its gap displays as `$0`), the monthly MAX DD reads `$0` with the zero class, and the chart tooltip shows no "Below peak:" line. `RiskMetrics.buildCumulativeTotalPnlSeries(historicalPnl, live)` is the one builder of the anchored series, and every consumer passes the same `live` through it: Max Drawdown (`histPnlDrawdown`), Current Drawdown (`histPnlCurrentDrawdown`), Drawdown Periods (`histPnlDrawdownEvents`), Recovery Factor, the current month's PROFIT and MAX DD (`histPnlMonthly`, `renderTables(..., live)`) and the cumulative chart's last candle (`AppCharts.pnl.render(historicalPnl, live, historyCut, gap)`). The time-weighted returns behind Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar and VaR stay on the rows (from inception when they reach it). The card value carries the loss class; the Max and Current Drawdown cards render through `AppPanels.overview.renderDrawdownCards(tsd, cdd, { source, hasLive, gap, closedCount, seriesStart })`, where `gap` (the trade path's `incompleteReason`, or `historyCut` on the historical-pnl path) blanks both cards with that reason; the Max Drawdown, Current Drawdown and Recovery Factor tooltips (`#maxDrawdownCard`, `#currentDrawdownCard`, `#recoveryFactorLabel`) follow `source`: on `'trade'` they describe cumulative closed-trade profit (net of fees, excluding funding and unrealized profit, numerator and denominator both from it), on `'hist'` they read the markup's own text, which describes the totalPnl curve and the live point; `AppPanels.risk.renderDrawdownPeriods(positions, historicalPnl, live, gap)` takes the same gap. The Max Drawdown detail reads "Peak +$X (YYYY-MM-DD) → trough -$Y (YYYY-MM-DD) · series since YYYY-MM-DD", the trough dated by `troughAt` and the first `/historical-pnl` row as its own phrase.

`RiskMetrics.drawdownSource(historicalPnl)` decides the series once for every drawdown card: `'hist'` whenever `/historical-pnl` has a row (an unreadable one included: the views then read `historicalPnlRowGap`), `'trade'` only when historical-pnl is unavailable or empty. A series that never draws down stays on `'hist'` and reads `$0` with "No drawdown recorded" (its true drawdown), never the closed-trade ledger's. On `'trade'` the cards use trade-system $ DD on cumulative fill-attributed `profit` (`RiskMetrics.tradeSystemDrawdown(positions, opts)`); a complete series that never draws down reads `$0` with "No drawdown recorded" there too, and `—` ("No closed trades") only without a closed trade. `opts` is `{ fills, nowMs }`, the same for `tradeSystemDrawdown`, `tradeSystemDrawdownEvents` and `tradeSystemCurrentDrawdown` (`processData()`'s `tradeSeriesOpts`: the fetched fills and `snapshotAtMs`): closes in one millisecond enter the series in their closing fills' chain order (the order every FIFO walk sorts fills in, `compareFillsChronologically` stable on the fetched order), never in the order the positions are listed, and the series ends at a point dated `nowMs` holding the last close's value, so an ongoing drawdown's time under water runs to now as it does to the live point on `'hist'`. The fallback is all-or-nothing like the classifier: while any closed position is incomplete (or the CLOSED list is unavailable), or any CLOSED position has no parseable `closedAt` (its place in the series is unknown; never dropped, never sorted last), the cumulative series is empty, so Max Drawdown, Current Drawdown and Recovery Factor render `—` with the drawdown gap, the classifier's `incompleteReason`, else `RiskMetrics.closeTimeGap(positions)` ("1 closed position without a valid close time"; `drawdownGap` in `index.html`), and the Drawdown Periods table holds only that reason. The series adds the profits exactly (decimal sums, as `exactSum`) and hands each close's cumulative value out as the Number nearest that decimal at 15 significant digits, so `+64.302905` then `-63.802905` stands at `0.5` (displayed `+$1`, a drawdown of `-$63`), never the float `0.4999999999999929`. The Recovery Factor and Drawdown Periods table follow the same fallback rule so all drawdown-derived cards stay aligned.

The Drawdown Periods table lists every peak→trough→recovery event on the same anchored `totalPnl` curve via `RiskMetrics.histPnlDrawdownEvents(historicalPnl, live)` (with the same `drawdownSource` fallback, `tradeSystemDrawdownEvents(positions, opts)`), deepest first by the depth it displays (`RiskMetrics.deeperDrawdownFirst`, see One displayed drawdown); the table scrolls and its title counts the events (`Historical Drawdown Periods (N, deepest first)`). **Recovery.** A drawdown ends (`recoveryAt`) at the first point whose gap to its peak displays as `$0` (`RiskMetrics.regainsPeak`: `Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peak, point)` not `Format.displaysAsLoss`), the rule by which Current Drawdown reads at peak, whether that point regains the raw peak or not, and the peak restarts from that point, so a later dip is its own drawdown (hourly `0, 100, 50, 99.8, 60, 120` holds two: `$50` recovering at 99.8, then `$40` from 99.8 to 60). The scan (`scanDrawdownEvents`) applies it on every path: `/historical-pnl`, the closed-trade fallback and each month's MAX DD; the chart's "Below peak:" line, measured from the displayed running peak, reads the same depths. Each event carries `endAt`: its `recoveryAt`, or the series' last point while ongoing. DURATION is the time under water, peak → `endAt` (peak → now on the ongoing row, the Current Drawdown card's days); TO TROUGH is peak → trough followed by the trough's UTC date (`11h 0m · 2025-01-01`); RECOVERY is trough → recovery (`—` while ongoing). Each header's tooltip says so. All three render through `Format.formatDuration`, so sub-day spans keep their hours (`10h 59m`, `1d 3h`) instead of rounding to whole days. The Monthly Performance Breakdown's MAX DD column uses the same scanner from the prior month-end (see Monthly Performance Breakdown), so it shares the headline's definition.

The Recovery Factor card is the latest value of the series the headline drawdown was measured on ÷ that drawdown as the Max Drawdown card displays it (`Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peakValue, troughValue)`), the numerator taken from the Current Drawdown result on the same source (`cdd.currentValue`): the live point (else the latest `totalPnl` row) on the historical-pnl path, cumulative closed-position `profit` on the fallback path. The numerator and denominator never come from different profit series. Its label's tooltip (`#recoveryFactorLabel`) names that numerator per path (`renderDrawdownCards`): the markup text (the live point, the Total Profit headline) with a live point; without one, the last hourly `totalPnl` row, because Total Profit is unavailable; on `'trade'`, closed-trade profit.

### Current Drawdown
`$ DD = max(0, peak − latest)` on the same anchored `totalPnl` series as Headline Max Drawdown, displayed as the displayed peak less the displayed latest value (`Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peakValue, currentValue)`, see Headline Max Drawdown, One displayed drawdown), so `latest` is the live point whenever it is computable — the peak of cumulative trading P&L excluding deposits and withdrawals, not the equity peak (the card tooltip says so). The two cards always share their P&L stream so they cannot disagree about which side of the ledger is being measured. `0` means the account is at-or-above its prior peak; positive value means the account has given back that many dollars since the peak. Helper: `RiskMetrics.histPnlCurrentDrawdown(historicalPnl, live)` returns `{ dollarDrawdown, pctOfPeakProfit, peakAt, peakValue, currentAt, currentValue, n, hasData }`. `peakAt` is the LAST point that regained the peak (`RiskMetrics.regainsPeak`, where the ongoing drawdown event starts), and `peakValue` the series' value there, so the card's days below peak equal the ongoing row's DURATION in Drawdown Periods.

Falls back to `RiskMetrics.tradeSystemCurrentDrawdown(closedPositions, opts)` (cumulative fill-attributed `profit`, ending now: `currentAt` is `opts.nowMs`, so "Nd below peak" runs to now, not to the last close) using the same `RiskMetrics.drawdownSource` decision as Max Drawdown — if the hist path was skipped for the max card, it's skipped for the current card too. `hasData: false` on an empty series → render `—` with "No equity series available".

Visual states on the card:
- a drawdown gap (the classifier's `incompleteReason`, else `RiskMetrics.closeTimeGap`, on the fallback path; `historicalPnlRowGap`, else `historyCut`, on the historical-pnl path) → `—`, detail the reason
- `hasData = false` → `—`, detail "No equity series available" (or "No closed trades")
- the displayed drawdown (`Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peakValue, currentValue)`) is `$0` (`Format.displaysAsLoss` false) → `$0`, green (profit class), detail "At peak (YYYY-MM-DD)": being at peak is good news, so this `$0` is the one drawdown zero that is not neutral (see Sign convention)
- otherwise → `-$X`, red (loss class), detail "2d 14h below peak (since YYYY-MM-DD) · Y.Y% of peak"
- on the closed-trade fallback (`'trade'`), the at-peak and below-peak details open with "Closed-trade profit · ", naming the series as the Max Drawdown caption does ("Trade-system peak-to-trough · …")
- on the historical-pnl path without a live point (Total Profit unavailable), either detail continues " · as of YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC, last hourly row (Total Profit unavailable)"

The time below peak is `Format.formatDuration(currentAt − peakAt)`, the same span in the same units as the ongoing Drawdown Periods row's DURATION. The "% of peak" (`pctOfPeakProfit`) is the depth the card shows over the peak as it displays, both at whole dollars (`Format.drawdownAsDisplayed(peak, current) ÷ Format.asDisplayed(peak, WHOLE_DOLLARS) × PERCENT`), so peak `+$1.40` and now `-$0.40` read `-$1` and `100.0% of peak`, never the raw `128.6%`. It shows only when the peak displays as a gain at whole dollars: a peak that reads `$0` on the Max Drawdown card (or a loss) has no profit to denominate against. That rule has one definition, `pctOfPeakAsDisplayed` (private to `risk-metrics.js`), which makes `pctOfPeakProfit` `null` otherwise (and without a series); the card shows the percent exactly when it is not `null`.

### Drawdown of arbitrary equity series
Use `RiskMetrics.validDrawdownFromEquity(equityArray)` for the Calmar denominator on compounded wealth; `RiskMetrics.compoundReturns` (below) is its one caller and serves both the time-series and the per-trade Calmar. The headline drawdown does not use it. Returns `null` when peak ≤ 0 OR trough < 0 — these are synthetic-equity artifacts that arise when the inception-time principal proxy goes under zero (e.g. a wipe followed by a redeposit). Filtered drawdowns must render as `—`.

### Time-weighted returns, Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar
`RiskMetrics.timeWeightedReturnPoints(historicalPnl, historyCut)` turns consecutive `/historical-pnl` rows into Modified Dietz returns, each `{ r, from, t, intervalMs }` (the rows it starts and ends at and the period's length; `computeTimeWeightedReturnsFromHist` gives the returns alone), with the period's inflow at full weight: `r_t = (totalPnl_t − totalPnl_{t−1}) / (equity_{t−1} + max(0, netTransfers_t))`. `netTransfers` is per row (the transfers since the prior row), so the hour after a deposit onto an empty account counts, and an outflow never shrinks the base. The rows are taken in order of their parsed `createdAt`, and there are no returns while a row is unreadable (see Headline Max Drawdown, Unreadable rows). Rows that reach inception (`historyCut` '', the second argument of `timeWeightedReturnPoints` and `timeWeightedReturnPointsByMonth`) whose first row already holds a profit or loss open with the inception period as its own return, `totalPnl_0 ÷ (0 + max(0, netTransfers_0))` (the dust floor applies), dated at the first row like the inception value of the drawdown series; its length is unknown (`/transfers` is not fetched), so the VaR sample leaves it out and `historicalPnlYears` does not count it. A period whose base is below `TWR_MIN_BASE_SHARE_OF_MEDIAN_EQUITY` (1%) of the median positive equity of the rows is dust (e.g. the hour after a near-total withdrawal) and is excluded, which lowers the adequacy gate's coverage; a base ≤ 0 is always excluded. The monthly SHARPE column reads `RiskMetrics.timeWeightedReturnPointsByMonth(historicalPnl, historyCut)` (`timeWeightedReturnsByMonth` gives the returns alone): the account's one return series (one dust floor, from the account's median) bucketed by the UTC month of the row each return ends at, so a month's returns are the periods ending in it, the one from the prior month's last row included, as in its PROFIT. A return is kept only when its start row is in the same month or the one just before (the rule `histPnlMonthly` applies to PROFIT), so a return spanning a month without rows counts in no month, as the next month's PROFIT is unknown.

Sharpe and Sortino stay on the arithmetic mean (`computeAnnualizedFromReturns`, √ periods-per-year), per sampling interval of the rows: on the time-series path (the account cards and the monthly SHARPE) each return weighs as the intervals it spans, k = its period ÷ the rows' median interval (`timeWeightedReturnPoints` / `timeWeightedReturnPointsByMonth` give each return's `intervalMs`, passed as `computeAnnualizedFromReturns(returns, timestamps, { intervalsMs })`; the actual ratio, no floor), read as r ~ N(μ·k, σ²·k): the per-interval mean is μ = Σr ÷ Σk, the variance σ² = Σ (r − μ·k)² ÷ k ÷ (n − 1) (weighted least squares) and Sortino's downside deviation √(Σ min(0, r)² ÷ k ÷ n) (target 0 per interval), so a return over a multi-hour or multi-day gap in the rows is not read as a one-interval outcome, and the same process reads alike whether its rows are hourly or partly daily. The inception period, of unknown length, counts as one interval (k = 1). The per-trade ratios pass no lengths, so every k is 1 and they are the plain mean over the sample standard deviation. `RiskMetrics.computeSharpe` and `computeSortino` are `null` when the returns have no spread: every excess return per interval (r ÷ k) stands for the same decimal at `AppConstants.NOISE_FREE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` (the display core's noise floor), so equal non-zero returns, whose float mean leaves a standard deviation of ~1e-18, never read a ratio of ~1e15; every Sharpe / Sortino reading them (the cards, the monthly and asset SHARPE, which read `—` with "Returns have no spread") follows; on either path, time-series or per-trade fallback, a null Sharpe or Sortino card takes that reason as its caption and tooltip (`AppPanels.performance.renderRatioMeta`, the one setter of both captions). Because a positive arithmetic mean can sit beside a compounded loss (volatility drag), the Sharpe and Sortino captions show the compounded return over the sample (`Time-weighted · hourly · n=… · compounded -10.0%`), and the card tooltips say that below 0 means the mean hourly return is negative and that above 0 does not mean the account made money. Every per-row SHARPE cell that shows a value does the same in its tooltip: "Compounded over the trades (return on equity at open): +X.X%" (Performance by Asset, `perTradeRatios(...).compounded`) or "Compounded over the month's time-weighted returns: X.X%" (Monthly, `compoundReturns(monthReturns).compounded`), then "SHARPE is the arithmetic mean of the returns ÷ their standard deviation: above 0 does not mean the row made money."; both header tooltips carry the caveat. Every compounded return reads through `AppPanels.performance.compoundedReturnText` (signed percent at one decimal, `—` once wealth reached zero), the card captions included. Neither the Sharpe nor the compounded return need agree in sign with the row's dollar PROFIT.

Calmar = CAGR ÷ max drawdown (%) of the compounded curve, via `RiskMetrics.compoundReturns(returns, years)` → `{ compounded, cagr, maxDrawdownPct, calmar }` (`cagr = W^(1/years) − 1`; `calmar` null without elapsed time, a positive wealth, or a drawdown). A drawdown that displays as `0.0%` at the caption's `RiskMetrics.CALMAR_DRAWDOWN_DECIMALS` (1) is no drawdown: `maxDrawdownPct` and `calmar` are null, as for a curve that never drew down, so a `-1e-9` return never yields a ratio in the millions. On the time-series path `years = RiskMetrics.historicalPnlYears(historicalPnl)`, the calendar time from the first to the last row with hours without capital counted, not `assessAdequacy`'s `years` (valid returns only). The card tooltip says it is annualized. Once the compounded wealth reaches zero or below (a return of −100% or worse) all four fields are null, and the Sharpe / Sortino captions read `compounded —`. `AppPanels.performance.renderCalmar(compounding, curve, reason)` renders the card on both paths with a caption naming the drawdown it divides by (without a result, `—` with `reason`, the Sharpe caption's, as caption text and tooltip; with a result whose compounded curve has no drawdown, `—` with `RiskMetrics.NO_DRAWDOWN_REASON`, "No drawdown recorded", the same way), `CAGR ÷ max DD of compounded TWR (X.X%)` (per-trade: `of compounded trade returns`), never the Max Drawdown card's dollars.

### Sample-adequacy gate
A statistical metric (Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, VaR, CVaR, monthly Sharpe) renders `—` unless the time-weighted-return sample passes ALL three constants (private to `risk-metrics.js`; `assessAdequacy` and `assessMonthAdequacy` read them, and `perTradeRatios` reuses the one-month span):
- `ADEQUACY_MIN_RETS = 30` (at least 30 valid TWR returns)
- `ADEQUACY_MIN_YEARS = 1 / MONTHS_PER_YEAR` (at least one month of valid coverage)
- `ADEQUACY_MIN_COVERAGE = 0.5` (at least half the historical-pnl rows produced a valid return — protects against post-wipeout sample bias)

Helper: `RiskMetrics.assessAdequacy(returns, timestamps, histLength)` → `{ adequate, reason, ppy, years, coverage, n }`. The numbers in a gate's reason are truncated, never rounded, through one helper (`truncatedText`, private to `risk-metrics.js`): a span reason (here and in `perTradeRatios`) gives the months to one decimal, so a span just short of the minimum reads "have 0.9 months", never "have 1.0 months" against "Need ≥1 month"; a coverage reason (account `Coverage 49% — …`, month `Returns cover 49% of the month (need ≥50%)`) gives a whole percent, so 49.7% never reads as the 50% it fails. The same gate fires for Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar AND VaR/CVaR — the dashboard never shows VaR for a sample window that's too short to support Sharpe.

The Monthly Performance Breakdown's SHARPE column uses `RiskMetrics.assessMonthAdequacy(returns, timestamps, histLength, monthKey)` → `{ adequate, reason, ppy, monthCoverage, coverage, n }`: the same return-count and row-coverage rules, but in place of the one-month span (which no complete 28-, 29- or 30-day month can meet at hourly sampling) the valid returns must cover at least `ADEQUACY_MIN_COVERAGE` of the month's own calendar periods at the sampling interval the timestamps show. At hourly sampling a complete month of any length passes, and the current month once half its hours are covered; the `ADEQUACY_MIN_RETS` minimum still applies, so at daily sampling a complete 28- to 30-day month holds fewer than 30 returns and reads `—` with "Need ≥30 returns". A gated cell renders `—` with the reason as its tooltip; a month that passes with returns that are all alike (idle funded equity) has no standard deviation and reads `—` with "Returns have no spread", the reason the Performance-by-Asset SHARPE gives for trade returns that are all alike and the Sharpe and Sortino cards give, as caption and tooltip, when the account's returns pass the time-series gate but are all alike (`AppPanels.performance.NO_SPREAD_REASON`).

When the time-series gate fails, Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar fall back to `AppPanels.performance.computeTradeBasedMetrics(cls, historicalPnl)` (a `classifyClosed` result), and the Performance-by-Asset SHARPE column (`assetTradeSharpe(cls, minSampleSize, historicalPnl)`, private to `src/panels/performance.js`, minimum `TUNABLES.ASSET_SHARPE_MIN_N`) is the same statistic per market. Both read `RiskMetrics.perTradeRatios(decisivePositions, historicalPnl, minTrades)` → `{ ok, reason, n, returns, yearsSpan, tpy, sharpe, sortino, calmar, compounded }`: returns on equity at open (`tradeReturnsOnEquity`, see Per-trade return) in closing order; `yearsSpan` from the first to the last close; Sharpe and Sortino scaled by √tpy with `tpy = (n − 1) / yearsSpan` (n − 1 close-to-close intervals); Calmar, its `maxDrawdownPct` and the compounded return from `compoundReturns` over `calmarYears`, the calendar time the returns accrued in, from the earliest open to the last close. It returns `{ ok: false, reason }`, rendered `—` with the reason (the fallback's meta caption, the SHARPE cell's tooltip), with a trade whose `closedAt` does not parse ("1 of 4 trades without a valid close time": every decisive trade reaches it, `decisiveByCloseTime` sorting such a trade last rather than dropping it, so no ratio is taken over the rest), fewer than `minTrades` decisive trades (never fewer than `MIN_TRADE_RETURNS = 2`), a trade without fill attribution or equity at open, or closes spanning less than `ADEQUACY_MIN_YEARS`: an annualized column never shows an unannualized or extrapolated value. Like the classifier it is all-or-nothing: while the classifier's `incompleteReason` is set the callers return `—` with that reason. The fallback caption reads `Per-trade · n=… · compounded …`, and `AppPanels.performance.renderRatioCardTitles(source)` gives the Sharpe and Sortino cards (`#sharpeCard`, `#sortinoCard`) tooltips describing per-trade returns on equity at open on `'trade'` (`renderTradeBasedRatios` calls it), and their markup text, which describes the time-weighted returns, on every other path (`processData()` calls it with `'hist'`). VaR/CVaR have no fallback — they render `—` with the gate reason.

**VaR / Expected Shortfall.** `RiskMetrics.historicalVaR(returns, subaccount)` → `{ varReturn, esReturn, varLoss, esLoss }`: the 5th-percentile TWR return (`sorted[floor(0.05·n)]`) and the Acerbi-Tasche discrete Expected Shortfall, the mean of exactly the worst 5% (with `k = 0.05·n`, `(Σ sorted[0..⌊k⌋−1] + (k − ⌊k⌋)·sorted[⌊k⌋]) / k`, so a return past the tail never dilutes it), as dollar losses `max(0, −return) × usableEquity`, or `null` without returns or usable equity (a negative equity would flip the sign into a green gain). `AppPanels.risk.renderFromHistorical(returns, subaccount, { label, ppy })` renders them as negative dollars; a loss that displays as `$0` (`Format.displaysAsLoss` false: the tail is gains, or a loss under half a dollar) reads `$0`, uncoloured, with a "no loss" caption. The sample is `RiskMetrics.varSampleReturns(historicalPnl)`: the time-weighted returns whose period lies within `VAR_MAX_INTERVAL_MULTIPLE` (1.5) of the rows' median sampling interval either way (from the median ÷ 1.5 to the median × 1.5), so neither a return over a multi-hour or multi-day gap nor one over an hour inside daily sampling stands in for a one-interval outcome (the value tooltip says so), and the count and span rules below count one-interval outcomes only. That sample must pass the Sharpe gate's count and span rules on its own: at least `ADEQUACY_MIN_RETS` returns, spanning at least `ADEQUACY_MIN_YEARS` at the rows' `ppy` (n ÷ ppy, as `assessAdequacy` measures it). Rows sampled hourly while the account was empty and daily once funded pass the gate on all their returns yet leave few one-period outcomes, covering hours rather than a month, so `renderFromHistorical` reads `—` with `RiskMetrics.varSampleGap(returns, ppy)` ("Only 1 return spans one sampling period (need ≥30)", or for 31 hourly returns "Need ≥1 month of valid data (have 0.0 months)") rather than a VaR of a few hours. The horizon is one sampling period of the returns, never scaled: the captions (`#var95Detail`, `#expectedShortfallDetail`) open with the same hourly / daily / periodic label the Sharpe meta uses (from `assessAdequacy`'s `ppy`), and the value tooltip names the period count per year. `AppPanels.risk.renderVarUnavailable(reason)` renders both `—` with the reason as tooltip and restores the markup captions.

### Cross-margin liquidation price
dYdX is cross-margin: the account is liquidated when total account value falls below the TOTAL maintenance margin requirement, `Σ |S_j| × P_j × MMF_j` over every open position. Per-position liq price holds every OTHER open position at its current oracle price (its unrealized profit AND its maintenance margin requirement `R = Σ_{j≠i} |S_j| × O_j × MMF_j`) — exact for single-position accounts. Derivation: at liquidation, equity plus the position's price move equals its own requirement at the liquidation price plus `R`.
- LONG:  `P_liq = (size × oracle − equity + R) / (size × (1 − MMF))`, floored at 0
- SHORT: `P_liq = (equity − R + oracle × |size|) / (|size| × (1 + MMF))`

Helper: `RiskMetrics.crossMarginLiqPrice(position, subaccount, marketsMap, positions)` returns `null` when MMF / size / oracle / equity is unavailable, or when another OPEN position's maintenance margin is unknown: its size is absent or not wholly numeric (`RiskMetrics.decimalNumberOf`; a reported `'0'` is a known zero and adds no margin), or its oracle or MMF is missing. `positions` is the account's position list; CLOSED rows and the position's own market are skipped (one open position per market). Omitting it treats the position as the account's only one. A SHORT whose numerator `equity − R + oracle × |size|` is not positive has no liquidation price (`null`): the account is below its total maintenance margin at every price. A LONG whose numerator `size × oracle − equity + R` is not positive is floored at `0`: no price above $0 liquidates it. `RiskMetrics.liquidationRow` names each case in `liqState`, a value of the frozen `RiskMetrics.LIQUIDATION_STATE`: `AT_PRICE` (`liq` is the price), `BELOW_MAINTENANCE` (a SHORT; `liq` and `distancePct` null), `NO_PRICE_LIQUIDATES` (a LONG; `liq` null, `distancePct` the full fall to $0, 100%), `UNKNOWN` (one of the position's own inputs is missing) or `OTHER_MARGIN_UNKNOWN` (its own inputs are there, but another OPEN position's maintenance margin is unknown). On the two unknown states `missing` lists what is lacking as `{ market, input }`, `input` a phrase of the frozen `RiskMetrics.LIQUIDATION_INPUT` (`market` null for an account or position input): on `UNKNOWN` the position's first missing input in the order equity (`EQUITY`, `RiskMetrics.EQUITY_UNAVAILABLE`), size, side, oracle price (`ORACLE`, `RiskMetrics.NO_ORACLE_PRICE`, the wording `unpricedReason` uses too), maintenance margin fraction; on `OTHER_MARGIN_UNKNOWN` every other OPEN position whose size (`SIZE`) or, failing that, oracle price or, failing that, maintenance margin fraction is missing; `[]` on the other states. The Liquidation table renders `BELOW_MAINTENANCE` as LIQ PRICE and DISTANCE `—` with "Below maintenance margin at every price" on hover and RISK SCORE BREACHED (it is already liquidatable), and `NO_PRICE_LIQUIDATES` as LIQ PRICE `—` with "No liquidation price: the account stays above maintenance margin even at a price of $0" on hover beside DISTANCE `+100.0%` and LOW, never a `$0` liquidation price. A row whose liquidation is unknown (the two unknown states, or no subaccount) reads LIQ PRICE, DISTANCE and RISK SCORE `—` with its reason on hover (private `unknownLiquidationReason`): each missing input, a market's followed by " for <market>", or the markets endpoint's missing-input reason in its place (`renderLiquidationTable`'s trailing `marketsGap`), so "No oracle price for SOL-USD" on the SOL-USD row; on `OTHER_MARGIN_UNKNOWN` followed by ": its maintenance margin is unknown" ("their maintenance margins are" for several markets), so a BTC row beside an unpriced SOL-USD position reads "No oracle price for SOL-USD: its maintenance margin is unknown"; without a subaccount, "Equity unavailable".

The Liquidation Risk Analysis table's LEVERAGE column shows `notional / subaccount.equity`. Per-position equity does not exist on the dYdX API. SIZE renders at full precision in base units (`Format.fmtAssetSize`: `1.5 BTC`). DISTANCE is signed, positive = room left: `(oracle − liq) / oracle` for a LONG, `(liq − oracle) / oracle` for a SHORT, rendered through `Format.fmtSignedPct` at `Format.PERCENT_DECIMALS` (`+10.0%`, `-0.1%`, `0.0%`). RISK SCORE classifies the distance as displayed (`Format.asDisplayed`): below zero the oracle is already past the liq price and it reads BREACHED (loss class), ahead of HIGH (< 10%), MEDIUM (< 20%) and LOW, so a distance that shows `+10.0%` is MEDIUM and BREACHED never sits beside `0.0%` (a distance that shows `0.0%` is HIGH). The LEVERAGE column and the Leverage card print at `LEVERAGE_DECIMALS` (2) and the card's tier (≥ 5x loss, ≥ 2x warning, else profit) reads the leverage as displayed, so `2.00x` is never conservative. The tiers are one list in `src/panels/risk.js` (`LEVERAGE_TIERS`, from `HIGH_LEVERAGE` and `ELEVATED_LEVERAGE`): the card's tone and its tooltip (`#leverageUtilCard`: the markup text followed by "5x or more = high; 2x or more = elevated; below 2x = conservative (leverage as displayed).") both read it.

**Missing OPEN list.** `renderLiquidationTable` and `renderLeverageUtilization` in `src/panels/risk.js` take `processData()`'s `openPositionsGap` (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Load cycle and application state) after their data arguments (`renderLiquidationTable` then takes `marketsGap`). While it is set, the Liquidation table holds a single full-width row with the reason, so an unknown list never reads as an account without open positions.

**Leverage card states.** The private `leverageReading` in `src/panels/risk.js` picks the value and its `#leverageUtilDetail` caption, first match wins:
1. `openPositionsGap` set → `—`, caption the reason (even when some OPEN rows are present, and even when equity is also missing)
2. `RiskMetrics.usableEquity(subaccount)` null → `—`, caption "Equity unavailable"
3. no OPEN position → `0.00x`, caption "No open positions"
4. an OPEN position `RiskMetrics.positionNotional` cannot value → `—`, caption `processData()`'s `openNotionalGap` (the markets endpoint's missing-input reason, or "No oracle price for A, B" via `unpricedReason`; "Open position notional unavailable" for a position without a size), even beside priced ones: a sum over the others would understate leverage
5. otherwise `RiskMetrics.leverageUtilization` under the markup's default caption ("Notional ÷ equity"), which also returns once a later load clears the reason.

**Usable equity** has one definition, `RiskMetrics.usableEquity(subaccount)`: the subaccount's equity when positive, else `null` (no subaccount, missing, not wholly numeric (`'5abc'`, read as the money amounts are, never `$5`), zero or negative). `crossMarginLiqPrice`, `leverageUtilization`, `liquidationRow`'s `lev`, `historicalVaR` (VaR / ES) and the Leverage card all gate on it.

**Open notional** has one definition, `RiskMetrics.positionNotional(position, marketsMap)` = `|size| × RiskMetrics.positionOraclePrice(position, marketsMap)`, the oracle being `position.oraclePrice || marketsMap[market].oraclePrice` (mark-based, matches dYdX's official UI; position objects from `/perpetualPositions` do **not** carry `oraclePrice`, which lives on `marketsMap[market].oraclePrice`); `null` without a positive size or an oracle price. There is no entry-price fallback: an entry-priced figure under the cards' oracle label would be a wrong number, so the cards read `—` with `openNotionalGap` instead, and `leverageUtilization` is `null` while any open position has no notional. It needs no subaccount. `RiskMetrics.leverageUtilization(positions, subaccount, marketsMap)` (the Risk-tab Leverage card: Σ notional ÷ equity), `RiskMetrics.liquidationRow(position, subaccount, marketsMap, positions)` (per row: `{ size, entry, oracle, notional, lev, liq, distancePct }`) and the Positions board's Active Positions card all use it, so they never diverge. `crossMarginLiqPrice`, `liquidationRow`'s `oracle` (0 without one), `notional`, `lev`, `liq` and `distancePct` (null without one) all need an oracle price. All are unit-tested in `test/risk-metrics.test.js`; the Risk panel's rendering of them (VaR / ES captions and the $0 floor, Drawdown Periods durations, Liquidation SIZE / BREACHED, the Leverage caption) in `test/risk-panel.test.js`.

### Behavior tab
`src/panels/behavior.js`; every figure is bucketed by entry (`createdAt`, UTC). Profit is the fill-attributed `profit` (net of fees, excluding funding) under the account classifier's all-or-nothing rule (see Trade classification).

- **Post-Loss Double Down.** Each complete CLOSED loser is paired with the next position in its market by `createdAt`, an OPEN one included (positions entered in one millisecond are taken in closing order, `RiskMetrics.byClosingOrder(fills)` with `processData()`'s `fillsArr`: by close, closes in one millisecond by their closing fills' chain order, OPEN last, as `attributeFillsToPositions` orders them, so the result never depends on the indexer's listing order, even for positions that share both their entry and their close millisecond); when the loser was closed by a reversal (`closedByFlip`) and that next position was opened by one (`openedByFlip`), it is the loser's own reversal and is skipped for the one after it. The pair counts when the successor is on the loser's side, entered within `TUNABLES.DOUBLE_DOWN_GAP_HOURS` of the loser's close, at ≥ `TUNABLES.DOUBLE_DOWN_SIZE_MULT` × the loser's `peakNotional`, both notionals and the product compared as the decimals they stand for (`Format.asDecimal`, the display core's `NOISE_FREE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS`), so a re-entry at exactly that multiple counts (1.8 ETH after 1.5 ETH at one price) whatever binary noise the products carry. An opposite-side re-entry (a reversal or a later re-entry the other way) is never a double down. FREQUENCY counts the successors, OPEN ones included; SUCCESS RATE and EXPECTANCY cover the closed ones. The detector can pair any position, so its count is unknown while any one is: the row is shown with FREQUENCY, SUCCESS RATE and EXPECTANCY `—` and the reason on hover while the classifier's `incompleteReason` is set, while `entriesGap` is (a list failed, or a position has no valid `createdAt`), while a CLOSED position has no valid close (`RiskMetrics.holdTimeGap`), or while an OPEN position lacks complete attribution ("1 open position missing fill data (<incompleteCause>)"): pairs with such a position cannot be judged and a count over the rest would be a lower bound. The row label's tooltip states the rule.
- **Long Hold** (> `TUNABLES.LONG_HOLD_HOURS`) and **Quick Flip** (< `TUNABLES.FLIP_HOLD_HOURS`) are hold-time buckets of closed positions on the hold as DURATION displays it (`Format.displayedDurationMs` of `RiskMetrics.holdMs`: a 4h 0m 20s hold reads `4h 0m` and is no Long Hold, a 14m 40s one reads `15m` and is no Quick Flip); their FREQUENCY needs no profit and stays numeric while positions are incomplete, but only while the closed-trade list itself is complete. While `closedTradesGap` is set (the CLOSED list failed, or an unlisted trade makes it short) or a CLOSED position has no hold window (`RiskMetrics.holdTimeGap`), both rows stay in the table with FREQUENCY, SUCCESS RATE and EXPECTANCY `—` and that reason on hover: a count over the rest would be a lower bound. Only a row whose count is known to be zero is left out. The avg hold time of wins / losses reads `—` with the same hold-window reason.
- **EXPECTANCY** column: the pattern set's classifier `expectancy`, average fill-attributed profit per decisive trade, net of fees, excluding funding (scratches and OPEN positions left out); the header tooltip says so.
- **SUCCESS RATE** tone: `Format.breakevenVerdict(winRate, breakevenWinRate, expectancy).toneClass` of the pattern set's own classifier, the Payoff captions' rule (see Trade classification), so a losing set's win rate never reads green beside its red EXPECTANCY; neutral when the set has no breakeven rate (no win or no loss). The header tooltip says so.
- **READING.** A description of the set's record, not trading advice: `—` with the reason while the set's ratios are unknown (the row's gap); else `Low sample` under `TUNABLES.PATTERN_MIN_N` decisive closed trades (the set classifier's `decisiveCount`: the wins and losses SUCCESS RATE and EXPECTANCY are measured on, so a set of scratches or open re-entries reads it at any FREQUENCY); else `Positive edge` when the expectancy displays positive and the win rate displays at least `POSITIVE_EDGE_MIN_WIN_RATE`, `Negative edge` when the expectancy displays negative and the win rate displays below `NEGATIVE_EDGE_BELOW_WIN_RATE`, else `Mixed`. Both are compared as displayed (`Format.asDisplayed(winRate, Format.PERCENT_DECIMALS)`, `Format.signClass(expectancy)` at whole dollars), so a `$0` expectancy is never a Negative edge and `55.0%` is never short of 55. The header tooltip states the rule, built from the constants.
- **Best / Worst Trading Hour.** The UTC entry hour with the highest / lowest mean profit per closed trade, among hours with ≥ `TUNABLES.HOUR_MIN_SAMPLE` trades. Each hour's profits are summed exactly (`RiskMetrics.exactSum`) and its mean taken as whole cents once from the exact quotient (`RiskMetrics.quotientCents(sum, n)`, never a float mean taken to the micro first) before the extremes, the ties and Worst-equals-Best are decided, so float residue never splits two equal means (a `$1.005` mean ties `$1.01`, and $5.024999 over 5 trades is `$1.00`, not `$1.01`). Hours tied at that mean are all named (`09:00 / 14:00 UTC`), with the tie on hover. Worst reads `—` when it would equal Best: only one hour qualifies, or every qualifying hour has the same mean (the reason is the tooltip). The label tooltips state the rule, built from the constant.
- **Position entries by hour and weekday (UTC).** The heatmap counts every position once, OPEN included, in the cell of its `createdAt`; a reversing order adds one entry, for the position it opens. While `entriesGap` is set it shows the reason instead of cells, including "N position(s) without a valid entry time" (`RiskMetrics.entryTimeGap`, any status), as Most Active Day does.

## Tax report (Portugal)

**Compute tax-year figures only through `TaxReport` (`tax-report.js`) and FX rates only through `FxRates` (`fx-rates.js`); never inline in `index.html` or in `src/panels/tax.js`.** When semantics change, update the helper AND this section in the same commit.

### Year boundary — realization by event date
Gains count when each operation happens (CIRS art. 10.º n.º 3), not when the position closes. Each fill portion's FIFO realized P&L and its fee (`portions` of the attribution; a reversing fill's fee split by size) count in the UTC year of that fill, opening fills' fees included, and each `/fundingPayments` row counts in the UTC year it was paid, for CLOSED and still-OPEN positions alike. Rows stay per position, but a position's row in year `Y` holds only its year-`Y` events, so a position partly closed in December and closed in January splits across the two years, and an open position's partial closes appear in their year marked `status: 'open'` (`TaxReport.ROW_STATUS`; the CLOSED column reads `open`). A position appears in `Y` exactly when it has a fill portion or a funding payment dated in `Y`; one whose attribution is incomplete has no trustworthy portions, so it also appears (reading `—`) in every year its window spans, an open window running to the latest year of any fill or payment. Years are UTC years, which equal mainland Portugal's civil year at the 31 Dec / 1 Jan boundary (WET = UTC+0; summer time WEST never spans New Year), and the viewer's browser timezone must not move events between years.

Helpers: `TaxReport.yearUTC(iso)` (`null` for invalid timestamps); `TaxReport.availableYears(positions, fills, fundingPayments)` lists every UTC year holding a fill, a payment, a close or a position's open, and every year an incomplete position's window spans (the rule `buildYearReport` places its `—` row by), newest first, so a year whose only realizations are partial closes is selectable, and so is every year an incomplete row reads `—` in (a position opened in a year with its opening fill missing included). Rows sort open first (newest opened first), then closed by `closedAt`, newest first, closes in one millisecond in the reverse of their closing fills' chain order (`RiskMetrics.byClosingOrder`), as on the Positions board.

**Reconciliation.** A year's totals equal the sum over that year's fills (realized, fees) and funding payments, and `summarize` shows a total only when that holds: a fill no position's attribution holds (`warnings.unattributedFillCount`; an unusable fill inside a listed position's window in its market, an undated one inside every window there, is left out, since that row is already incomplete for it) or a funding payment no listed position held (`warnings.unattributedFundingCount`), dated in the year or undated, blanks the totals that sum it (fills: net, gross, fees; funding: funding, net, gross), as an incomplete row does. The status line and warning strip name the count.

**Funding per payment.** `/fundingPayments` rows (the payments `processData()` already loaded, `fundingPaymentsInput` in `index.html`, the same input the Market tab reads) are tied to positions by private `attributeFundingPayments`: the position in the payment's market (`ticker`) whose window `[createdAt, closedAt]` (open-ended while OPEN) holds the payment's `createdAt`, on the payment's `side` when it names one (a payment at a reversal's instant goes to the side it names), the earliest-created when several qualify. Only an OPEN position's window is open-ended: a CLOSED position without a parseable `closedAt` could hold any payment in its market on its side at or after its `createdAt`, so such a payment cannot be placed. It counts as unattributed (`warnings.unattributedFundingCount`, blanking the funding, net and gross totals), and every row that could hold it (the undated CLOSED one and any other position whose window holds it on its side) appears in the payment's year with funding and net unknown (`_fundingMissing`, its reason in `_fundingMissingReason`, "A funding payment cannot be placed: a closed position has no valid close time", on the † tooltip and the warning strip; not exported), never on the earlier row with a known `$0.00` on the later one. A position's `netFunding` field is not used.

### Realized P&L and fee source — per-position fill attribution
`buildYearReport(positions, fills, fundingPayments, year, usdPerEurByDate)` takes each row's `realizedPnlUSD` and `feesUSD` as the sums of its year's `portions` from `RiskMetrics.attributeFillsToPositions`, run once over every position (OPEN included, so an open position's fills are never claimed by a closed one) and the entire fills array: one continuous FIFO walk per market, never restarted at position boundaries, so year totals reconcile to the equity curve. A reversing fill's realized goes to the position it closes and its fee is split pro-rata by size between the two positions (see Per-position attribution); both portions carry the reversing fill's date, so the split never moves fee between years.

**Incomplete attribution.** A row whose attribution is not `complete` carries `_attributionIncomplete: true` and `realizedPnlUSD = feesUSD = netUSD = null` (their EUR mirrors `undefined`): its number could hold another position's fills (e.g. the segment of a position missing from the list lands in the neighbour whose window contains it), or one of its fills has no fee (`UNKNOWN_FEE`; `_realizedFillError` `'attribution-incomplete'`), so a filing never understates its costs with a `$0` fee. `summarize` is all-or-nothing like the classifier: while any row is incomplete (`incompleteCount > 0`) the net, gross gains / losses and fee totals, USD and EUR, are `undefined` rather than a partial sum; funding and the row count stay. `warnings.incompleteAttributionCount` drives the status line and the warning strip, and the panel renders those cells, totals and the W / L / S split as `—`.

**Unknown funding.** When `/fundingPayments` did not load (`fundingPayments` is `null`), or one of a row's year payments has no parseable `payment` amount, or could be held by a CLOSED position without a parseable `closedAt` (see Funding per payment), the row reads `netFundingUSD = netUSD = null` (EUR mirrors `undefined`) with `_fundingMissing: true` (CSV `funding_missing`), never $0; realized and fees stay. A position with no payment in the year is a known zero. The † tooltip and the strip give the reason: the payments' missing-input reason, passed as `fundingGap`, else the row's `_fundingMissingReason`, else the unparseable amount. `summarize` counts such rows in `fundingMissingCount` and leaves the funding, net and gross gains / losses totals (USD and EUR) `undefined`, while the fee total stays; the W / L / S split counts only rows whose net is known, and the panel renders it `—` with "N rows missing funding" on the net cards, the status line and the warning strip.

**Net-based buckets.** Gross gains / losses (USD) and the W / L / S split bucket rows by the sign of `netUSD` at cents (realized − fees + funding; a net that shows `$0.00` is a scratch), while the dashboard's classifier buckets by fill-attributed profit, which excludes funding; the cards say so ("· net incl. funding", "by net incl. funding") so a trade whose funding flips its sign is not read as the same W / L on both tabs. The EUR sublines of the gross cards bucket each row by the sign of its own `netEUR`, which suits an EUR filing: each event converts at its own date's quote, so one row can be a USD gain and an EUR loss (a fee paid on a low-quote day outweighing a gain on a high-quote day). The two label tooltips say so.

The row's SIZE (PEAK) / ENTRY / EXIT (`peakSize`, `entryPrice`, `exitPrice`, also in the CSV / JSON) are the attribution's `peakSize` / `entryVwap` / `exitVwap`, the values the Positions board shows, and `null` while the attribution is incomplete (private `attributedSizeAndPrices`), even when the incomplete attribution carries segment values: on screen `—`, in the CSV empty cells, in the JSON `null`. On screen the size cell uses the board's formatter (`Format.fmtAssetSize`); prices of $1 and up keep up to 4 decimals (the board shows 6 significant digits), thousands grouped on screen (`Format.groupDecimalText`), because a tax filing needs them, rounded once at 4 decimals by the one display rounding (`TaxReport.priceText`: `Format.fmtFixed(price, 4)`, trailing zeros trimmed), so a VWAP whose exact value is a 4-decimal tie rounds up (the attribution's VWAP is the exact decimal at 15 significant digits, see Per-position attribution) and 100000.99504950495 reads `100000.995`, never `.9951` through a 12-digit rounding first; prices below $1 render through the board's `Format.formatPrice`, the one definition of a micro-price's precision. The CSV and JSON exports round all three to `AppConstants.SIZE_SIGNIFICANT_DIGITS` significant digits, rounded once by the same rule and written as that plain decimal text, never in exponent form (`Format.fmtSignificant`, through private `exportDecimalText`; the JSON writes the same text as a JSON number through `exportRow` and `toJson`; 64012.12345675, stored just below its tie, writes `64012.1234568`, a $0.000000406473 price `0.000000406473`), the same precision `fmtAssetSize` shows sizes at, so the float noise of summed fills (`0.30000000000000004`) stays out of the files; the row objects and the on-screen table are not rounded. The indexer's fields are not used as a substitute. SIZE (PEAK), ENTRY, EXIT and CLOSED are the whole position's, one definition shared with the board, across every year: a row of a position partly closed in December holds only December's realized, fees and funding beside an EXIT that includes January's sale. The four header tooltips say so, and a row whose position has fill portions or funding payments in more than one UTC year carries `_eventYears` (`{ first, last }`, private `eventYearSpan`; left out of both exports, so no schema change), from which the panel adds an audit hint to the date cell's tooltip and `aria-label`, without a †: "Position spans 2024–2025: SIZE (PEAK), ENTRY, EXIT and CLOSED are the whole position's; REALIZED, FEES and FUNDING hold only 2024's events."

**Inputs unavailable.** When `/fills`, the CLOSED list or the OPEN list did not load (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Missing inputs), `processData()` passes the first reason to the Tax panel (`tax.render(positions, fills, address, { dataGap, fundingPayments, fundingGap, snapshotAtMs })`, `snapshotAtMs` the time the data was read): without fills nothing can be computed, and without a position list fills cannot be tied to the right rows. Realized, fees, net and EUR net render `—` per row; the net / gross / fee totals and the W / L / S split render `—`; funding and the row count still show; the status line reads "Year Y · N rows · reason" (the open / closed split is itself unknown); the warning strip names the reason; nothing is downloadable and no FX request is made. With no year at all the status line shows the reason instead of "No fills or funding payments for this address."

Helper: `RiskMetrics.attributeFillsToPositions(positions, fills)` — see Per-position attribution.

**Row flags.** Incompleteness is carried once: `_attributionIncomplete` (CSV `attribution_incomplete`) is `true` exactly when the attribution is not complete (`RiskMetrics.hasCompleteAttribution`), and `_realizedFillError` (CSV `realized_fill_error`) is its reason, never judged from the indexer window alone: `null` when complete, otherwise the most specific of `'no-fills-in-window'` (no fill of the market inside the window `[createdAt, closedAt]`, open-ended while OPEN), `'invalid-fill-in-slice'` (a fill in that window fails `isFifoUsableFill`), else `'attribution-incomplete'`. `warnings.incompleteAttributionCount` counts the incomplete rows; the report's `warnings` holds only that, `feeAttributionAmbiguousCount`, `missingFxDates`, `unattributedFillCount` and `unattributedFundingCount`. A complete row is exact even when its window does not net flat in isolation: the two rows of a reversal both hold the reversing fill, yet each is complete and carries only the overlap hint (see Fee attribution). Because any incomplete row blanks the fills-derived year totals, a total that shows is a sum over complete rows only.

**Invalid fills.** The FIFO walk skips fills whose `price`/`size`/`side` won't parse (`RiskMetrics.isFifoUsableFill` is the one definition); `attributeFillsToPositions` marks the positions whose window holds one `complete: false`, so their rows read `—` and blank the totals as above. The row also carries `_hasInvalidFill` (the same predicate over its window's fills; CSV column `invalid_fill_in_window`), and the panel adds an invalid-fill note to that row's † tooltip; no warning counter tallies it. The status line and warning strip read `warnings.incompleteAttributionCount` only: an invalid fill always makes the row incomplete, so the incomplete-attribution message covers it.

**† marks warnings only.** The panel appends † to a row's date cell when the row has a warning: a data gap, incomplete attribution, unknown funding, an invalid fill, or a missing FX rate. Each warning's text goes in the cell's tooltip and `aria-label`. The same-market overlap (below) and a position spanning years (see SIZE (PEAK) above) are audit hints: their text joins the tooltip and `aria-label`, but they add no †.

`buildYearReport` always takes realized and fees from `RiskMetrics.attributeFillsToPositions`; nothing is computed per window. Batch path: fills pre-grouped by market and sorted once by `RiskMetrics.timestampMs(createdAt)`. Per-position window slicing, which feeds only `_hasInvalidFill` and the `'no-fills-in-window'` reason, uses binary search for `[lo, hi)` bounds (`O(log marketFills + windowSize)` per row). The fill attribution is built once per call (`O(n log n)`, where n is total fills, plus a linear scan of a market's positions for each segment whose first fill matches no position's `createdAt`). Overlap detection is a sort+sweep per market (`O(n log n)` sort, `O(n + total_overlaps)` sweep) that records every chain member (A↔B↔C with A and C not overlapping still marks all three).

### Fee attribution
Per-fill fees on `/v4/fills` are attributed by the **same fill attribution** as realized P&L: each fill portion belongs to exactly one position, and a reversing fill's fee is split by size between the position it closes and the one it opens. **No side filter** — `/v4/fills` exposes side as `BUY`/`SELL` while positions are `LONG`/`SHORT`, AND both sides legitimately belong to a position's lifecycle (BUY opens a LONG / closes a SHORT; SELL closes a LONG / opens a SHORT). A direct side equality check would never match a single fill.

When ANOTHER position in the **same market**, closed or OPEN, overlaps the window (regardless of side), the row carries `_feeAttributionWarning=true`. The sweep uses the same windows as funding attribution (private `positionWindow`: `[createdAt, closedAt]`, open-ended while OPEN), and runs over every listed position, not only the year's rows. Fees and realized P&L are still attributed uniquely by the fill attribution (no double-count of boundary fills), so the flag is only an audit hint that the windows touch, and the two rows of every reversal carry it, including a reversal into a still-open position (they share the reversing fill's instant). Despite its name it covers **realized P&L as well as fees**; the panel's status line reads "N rows overlap another position's window", the strip "… overlap another position's window in the same market, open or closed …", the row's date cell explains it in the tooltip without a †, and neither the status line nor the strip calls the row values approximate. Downstream UI / CSV / JSON treat it as a combined attribution hint.

### Net realized P&L (per row)
`netUSD = realizedPnlUSD + netFundingUSD − feesUSD`, `null` when any of the three is unknown (incomplete attribution, unknown funding). Fees always subtract because the attributed `realized` is gross of fees (the FIFO walk looks only at fill prices and sizes).

Helper: `TaxReport.netRealizedPnl(realizedPnlUSD, netFundingUSD, feesUSD)`, `null` when any of the three is not a finite number (an unknown amount is unknown, never `$0`).

### Cents: rows foot to their totals
Every Tax amount is at cents, rounded once in `TaxReport` with `RiskMetrics.wholeCents` (the rule `profitLedger` uses; see Exact sums, one rounding), so the screen, the CSV and the JSON carry the same figures and every column foots as displayed:
- a row's `realizedPnlUSD`, `netFundingUSD` and `feesUSD` are their exact sums (funding added as decimals, `RiskMetrics.exactSum`; fees as the exact sum of the portions' exact shares, `RiskMetrics.portionFeesTotal`, as is each `eventsByDate` entry's `feesUSD`) rounded to cents once, and `netUSD` is `realized + funding − fees` of those rounded parts;
- each `eventsByDate` entry's `realizedUSD` / `feesUSD` / `fundingUSD` is that date's exact sum at cents, so the JSON carries no float noise; these are the per-date figures EUR converts from, and need not add up to the row's USD, which rounds the year's sum once;
- EUR rounds per event date: each `eventsByDate` entry's `realizedEUR` / `fundingEUR` / `feesEUR` is its USD at cents ÷ quote rounded to cents once, half away from zero, from the exact quotient (`RiskMetrics.quotientCents`: the USD as the decimal it stands for over the quote as published, in integers, never a float quotient first rounded to the micro, so 48.01 ÷ 1.2001 = 40.00499… reads 40.00; a reader recomputes it from the entry), the row's EUR realized / funding / fees sum those cents, and `netEUR` derives from the row's rounded EUR parts (so a row's EUR can differ by a cent from its USD amount converted in one step);
- every `summarize` total (net, gross gains / losses, fees, funding, USD and EUR) sums the rows' whole cents, and the gross buckets read the sign of the row's net at cents.

So year totals reconcile to the year's fills and funding payments to within a cent per row rather than exactly.

### Currency conversion
EUR converts each event at the ECB daily reference rate of its own UTC date (CIRS art. 23.º): every fill portion's realized P&L and fee at its fill's date, every funding payment at its payment date (provider, request and cache behaviour: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Tax report and FX and Browser storage). Each row carries `eventsByDate`, its year's events summed per UTC date at cents (`{ date, realizedUSD, feesUSD, fundingUSD, usdPerEur, realizedEUR, feesEUR, fundingEUR }`), and `TaxReport.fxDates(rows)` lists the dates the panel asks `FxRates.getRates` for. The rate is the ECB's own EUR/USD quote, USD per 1 EUR as published, written onto each event as `usdPerEur`, and `EUR = USD / quote` per date at cents, summed (see Cents). Frankfurter's USD → EUR rate is that quote's inverse rounded to five decimals and misstates large amounts at cent level, so it is not used. Weekend/holiday dates inherit the nearest preceding business-day rate, at most the longest ECB closure (four days, Good Friday to Easter Monday) back; a date whose only quote is older reads missing. A Saturday or Sunday is final as soon as the quote answering it is the Friday just before, however recent; any other date answered by an earlier business day stays provisional (missing) until it is more than two days old (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, Tax report and FX).

When a rate is unavailable for any of a row's event dates, that row converts nothing (`_fxMissing: true`, the dates join `warnings.missingFxDates`) and its EUR cells render `—`, with the dates in the † tooltip — never a year-end or close-date fallback. `_fxConverted: true` marks a row whose EUR figures are final. EUR totals are all-or-nothing like the fills-derived ones: `summarize` returns every EUR total (net, gross gains / losses, fees, funding) as `undefined` unless every row converted, so no card shows a partial sum beside a USD total that covers every row, and the on-screen card never reads a misleading `€0.00`:
- every row converted (`eurComplete: count > 0 && eurMissingCount === 0`) → full EUR totals, each as known as its USD total: funding EUR shows even when every row's fill attribution is incomplete, while fees, net and gross EUR stay `undefined` with their USD totals.
- some rows converted, some not → `eurPartial: true`, every EUR total `undefined` (cards `—`; the Net EUR caption reads "unavailable: N rows missing FX").
- no row converted → every EUR total `undefined` (cards `—`; caption "unavailable", keyed off `eurComplete` in the panel).

`eurRowCount` counts the converted rows whose attribution is complete.

`convertRowsToEur` is idempotent: each call resets EUR fields, `_fxMissing` and `_fxConverted` on every row before re-applying so re-renders against fresh FX rates always produce a clean result.

Helper: `FxRates.getRates(dates: string[]) -> Promise<{rates, missing}>`, `rates` mapping each date to its EUR/USD quote.

### Classification
Perpetual futures are derivative operations, so their gains are mais-valias under Categoria G, CIRS art. 10.º n.º 1 e): `TaxReport.CLASSIFICATION` is that single category, labelled 'Categoria G — mais-valias, operações com instrumentos derivados (art. 10.º n.º 1 e) CIRS)', 28% flat, with the note 'Confirm the classification with an accountant.' shown on its card (`taxClassificationNote`) and written into both exports. There is no category choice and no holding-days column: Categoria E covers derivatives only for interest-rate swap gains, and the cripto-ativos rule (al. k) with its 365-day exclusion) is spot crypto, not perps.

### Sign convention
**Losses always render as negative dollars.** Helper: `formatCurrency(value)` with negative input. Avg Loss (the card, the Monthly AVG LOSS column and the Payoff caption's "avg win / avg loss") and the Profit Factor caption's and tooltip's gross loss must pass `-Math.abs(loss)` so the displayed sign matches the visual loss styling. TROUGH CUM. PROFIT in the Drawdown Periods table is a level on the cumulative curve, not a loss, and keeps its own sign. The Performance-by-Asset WORST column passes the losing trade's own `profit`, which is already negative.

**Zero is neutral.** A value that displays as zero (`$0`, `0.00%`, `$0.00`) carries no sign and no gain/loss colour. This includes the Market tab's current funding APR (`Format.fundingAprClass`) and the Tax tab's fee cells. The Tax tab is the one place fees are not a negative contribution: its "Fees paid" card and FEES PAID USD column keep the dYdX convention its exports use (positive = paid, unsigned and uncoloured; a maker rebate `-`), while the Overview ledger and Performance by Asset show fees as a signed contribution to profit. Every signed or signable formatter (`Format.formatCurrency`, `formatCents`, `fmtNotional`, `fmtSignedPct`, `formatFundingApr`, `fmtRatio`, `formatPercent`, `fmtNum`, `formatShortNumber`) decides the sign from the rounded value, so none prints a negative zero (`-0.00`, `-0.0%`, `-$0`); Kelly renders through `formatPercent` and the Liquidation DISTANCE through `fmtSignedPct` for the same reason, and `Format.signClass(value, fractionDigits = 0)` returns `'profit'` / `'loss'` / `'zero'` (muted ink) from the same rounding; every signed cell takes its class from it (`AppDom.updateMetric(id, text, toneClass)` for metric cards). A component that sets its own text colour (a metric card's value and caption, the Overview statistics column) would outrank the bare tone class, so the stylesheet's last rules repeat each tone for those components; a new component with its own colour that takes tone classes joins them. The drawdown cards follow it too: a drawdown that displays as `$0` is neutral (see Headline Max Drawdown, One displayed drawdown), with one exception: the Current Drawdown card is not a signed cell, and its `$0` "At peak" state is deliberately green (see Current Drawdown). Ratios (`Format.fmtRatio`) never print `-0.00`.

**One minus glyph, one grouping.** Every signed number on screen, in every unit, writes its minus as the hyphen-minus `-` (`Format`'s private `MINUS`), never U+2212. Every dollar amount on screen groups thousands (`+$1,235`, `-$72,410`, `$84,250`, `$64,012.1235`): `formatCurrency`, `formatCents`, `fmtNotional` and `formatPrice` share one grouping rule, and the Tax tab's 4-decimal prices are grouped by `Format.groupDecimalText` without re-rounding. The CSV and JSON exports are untouched: plain, ungrouped numbers.

**One payoff text.** The Payoff ratio reads `0.57 : 1` (`Format.fmtPayoff`) on the Overview hero, the Trading Statistics row, the Strategy Edge's implied payoff and the Performance card; each keeps its own placeholder (`—` / `N/A`) when the payoff is unavailable.

**Prices carry no sign.** A price is not a gain or a loss, so it renders through `Format.formatPrice` (`$65,000`), never `formatCurrency` (`+$65,000`). It keeps 6 significant digits at every magnitude, rounded by the one display rule (see One display rounding), always as a plain decimal (`$52,345`, `$2,543.27`, `$3.4`, `$0.0000012`, `$0.000000000406473`; never exponent form), so a market priced between $1 and a few hundred dollars does not lose its moves to whole-dollar rounding. This includes the funding chart's right axis and its tooltip's "Price:" line.

## Positions board

`src/panels/positions.js` renders the Positions tab from `allPositions` (see Per-position attribution); it recomputes no formula. The table lists the most recent `AppConstants.TUNABLES.RECENT_POSITIONS_CAP` closed positions, newest first.
- **CLOSED (UTC)** — `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM` in UTC (`Format.fmtDateTimeUTC`), the timezone the Tax tab assigns years in.
- **SIDE** — a `⇄` tag on rows opened or closed by a reversing order (`openedByFlip` / `closedByFlip`); its tooltip says the reversing fill's size and fee are split between both rows.
- **SIZE** — `peakSize` in base-asset units, full precision (`Format.fmtAssetSize`: `250 ETH`, `3.14159 BTC`). **ENTRY / EXIT** — `entryVwap` / `exitVwap`.
- **PROFIT** — `profit`, net of fees; funding is the separate FUNDING column (`netFunding`), so PROFIT + FUNDING = net. **PROFIT %** — `tradeReturn × 100`.
- Size, price, profit and return cells render `—` when the position's attribution is incomplete, with the fills' missing-input reason on hover when `/fills` did not load, else the position's `incompleteCause`. **FUNDING** renders `—` with `RiskMetrics.NO_NET_FUNDING` ("No netFunding reported") on hover when `RiskMetrics.positionNetFunding` finds none (a `'0'` is a known `$0`).
- Rows sort newest first in the one closing order, the reverse of `RiskMetrics.byClosingOrder(fills)` (closes in one millisecond by their closing fills' chain order, never the indexer's listing order); a row whose `closedAt` does not parse (`RiskMetrics.timestampMs`) sorts last.
- A CLOSED list that did not load (`closedGap`) fills the table with its reason as the only row, and the Hold Time Distribution shows it too, never zero counts. A CLOSED position without a valid hold window (`RiskMetrics.holdTimeGap`) also leaves the Hold Time Distribution with its reason, so the bins always add up to the closed count when shown. Fills holding a closed trade no listed position owns (`unlistedTradeGap`, see Per-position attribution) put that reason in a row above the listed positions, and the Hold Time Distribution and the Behavior tab's entry heatmap show it instead of counts that leave the trade out.
- **DURATION** — `Format.formatDuration` of `RiskMetrics.holdMs` (`96d 22h` / `5h 12m` / `12m`; `—` without a valid hold window), shared with the Behavior tab's hold times.
- **Active Positions** — count of OPEN positions; detail is the open notional, Σ `RiskMetrics.positionNotional` (the Risk tab's leverage definition; needs no subaccount), grouped with thousands separators. While `openPositionsGap` is set the count reads `—` and the detail is that reason; when any open position has no notional the detail is `openNotionalGap` (e.g. "No oracle price for SOL-USD"), or `—` for a position without a size.
- **Taker Share** (`#positionsTakerShare`) — taker fills ÷ all fills, by count, through `Format.formatPercent` like every percent (3 of 2000 reads `0.2%`); `—` with the fills' missing-input reason as its caption (`#positionsTakerShareDetail`) when `/fills` did not load. Each fill must carry `liquidity` `TAKER` or `MAKER`: a fill with any other or no flag has unknown liquidity, never a maker fill, and the share reads `—` with "N fill(s) without a liquidity flag" as its caption.
- **Hold Time / Position Size Distribution** — labeled rows (label, bar, count) from `renderHistogram`, readable at 390px. Hold-time bins take each hold as its DURATION displays it (`Format.displayedDurationMs`), each bin `[lo, hi)`, so `1h 0m` counts in `1–4h` and `7d 0h` in the open-ended last bin, labelled `≥7d`. Size bins use `RiskMetrics.peakNotional`, the same denominator as `tradeReturn`: equal-width bins from the smallest notional (the last open-ended), each notional binned as its label prints (`Format.displayedShortNumber`, the number `formatShortNumber` shows) against the edges as they print, so a notional on an edge, or one that prints as an edge label ($1,799 reads `$1.80K`), lands in the bin that label opens, labelled through `Format.formatShortNumber`, which picks K / M after rounding (`$1.00M`, never `$1000.00K`), fewer while two bin edges would print alike, and one bin labelled with the single notional when every trade prints alike. The Performance tab's Win/Loss Distribution uses the same primitive over the decisive trades (so its bins add up to the Win Rate card's wins and losses): 2%-wide return bins over ±20% with an edge at 0, each labelled by its range (`-2% to 0%`; the outer bins `< -18%` and `≥ 18%` take everything beyond), red below 0 and green from 0. Each trade is binned on its PROFIT % as the board displays it (`Format.asDisplayed` at `Format.TRADE_RETURN_DECIMALS`, the PROFIT % column's decimals), so an exact -14% (`-14.000000000000002` in binary) counts in `-14% to -12%` and an exact +14% in `14% to 16%`, but a decisive trade never crosses the 0 edge: a loss whose PROFIT % displays as `0.00%` (-$40 on $1M) counts in `-2% to 0%`, a win in `0% to 2%` or above.
